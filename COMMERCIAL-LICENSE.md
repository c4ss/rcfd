# Commercial licence

rcfd is free software under the [GNU AGPL v3](LICENSE). If you modify rcfd and let others use it over a network, or distribute
it, the AGPL requires you to offer your users the complete source under the same licence.

If that does not suit you (for example you want to embed rcfd in a closed-source product, or run a modified version as a
service without publishing your changes), you can buy a commercial licence that replaces the AGPL terms for your use. Terms
are agreed per customer.

To ask about one, open an issue titled "Commercial licence" on the repository, or contact the maintainer through the GitHub
profile of the repository owner.

Use under the AGPL, including commercial use, needs no permission and no payment.
