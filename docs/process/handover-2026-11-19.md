## For the maintainer (scope)

- **How far the citation work goes.** Batch 1 cost about 1.1 million tokens for six ADRs and 70 citations; 305 ADRs
  remain, so the whole is of the order of 50 million tokens. The weekly citation item of lead-process.md (five
  citations) is the alternative. Recommendation: the weekly item, and batches only for ADRs whose numbers the code
  uses and that mark a value UNVERIFIED.
- **Whether a fresh reader per item becomes a rule.** In the trial both models found the planted defects (5 of 6 and
  6 of 6) at about 1.5 million tokens for 8 items; about a third of what both readers agreed on was a false alarm, and
  the real findings were mostly defects of the record. Recommendation: no rule; a reader for items that change a
  default result or a golden.
