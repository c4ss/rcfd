# ADR thread: the structural solver plan, three tiers and their 66 work packages (ADRs 0270, 0290, 0310 and their stubs)

**Origin:** the ADRs in the table below, `docs/adr/` at `origin/main` 2e94e5ef.
**Kind:** ADR thread: three plan ADRs (status PROPOSED, 2026-11-12) and the 49 stubs of theirs that stayed PLANNED at the snapshot; 52 ADRs. The 17 plan numbers that were turned into registrations in place (0271, 0273 to 0279, 0281 to 0284, 0286, 0291, 0292, 0296, 0305) are on [[adr-thread-structural-basic-tier]].

On 2026-11-12 "The maintainer wants a structural solver, planned in three tiers: basic, intermediate, advanced" (ADR 0270). Each plan lays its tier out as numbered work packages, "each one work package for a single implementation agent", with a stub file per number at status PLANNED, and "the registration with its limits is written by the implementation agent before any code". The basic tier (ADR 0270, SB-1 to SB-18 = F-370 to F-387) is linear elastostatics and modes at rest of isotropic solids on Tet4, Hex8 and Tet10, with the loads a fan blade sees and one-way coupling. The intermediate tier (ADR 0290, SI-1 to SI-19 = F-388 to F-406) is "what a rotating thin blade needs (elements that bend, stress stiffening, spin softening, a Campbell diagram, cyclic symmetry, harmonic response), dynamics in time, the first two-way coupling with the flow solver, and the entry points". The advanced tier (ADR 0310, SA-1 to SA-29 = F-408 to F-436) covers materials, contact, fracture, rotordynamics, substructuring, sparse factorisation, ranks and device, and the 120 mm fan demonstration: "This is the last tier. No later planner exists, so what is not in the list of section 3 is not in the structural roadmap." See [[rcfd-fem]] and [[structural-solver]].

## Key numbers and limits

- **Architecture (ADR 0270 §2.1):** `rcfd-fem` depends on `rcfd-core`, `rcfd-mesh` and `rcfd-linalg` only, in layer 2 "below `rcfd-io`, `rcfd-fv` and `rcfd-post`"; generic numerics (direct solver, eigensolver, preconditioners) go into `rcfd-linalg`; the file writers live in `rcfd-io`, and the glue (write a result, read a deck, be a co-simulation participant) in `rcfd-post`.
- **Conventions for all tiers (ADR 0270 §2.2):** SI units; unknowns through a `DofMap`, "no code outside `DofMap` assumes three"; symmetric tensors as six numbers in the order xx, yy, zz, xy, yz, zx, "strains carry engineering shear (`gamma = 2 eps`), stresses do not"; "A pressure is positive when it presses on the solid"; "results are bit-identical for any thread count; assembly order is fixed by element index"; "Every refusal is an error with the element, node, set or key it concerns; nothing is repaired silently".
- **Basic-tier scope (ADR 0270 §1.1):** Tet10 is in the basic tier because "the tetrahedral mesher is the only route to a blade mesh, and the linear tetrahedron locks in bending"; the direct solver is the default "to a few times 1e4 unknowns", an estimate that is "arithmetic on the envelope size, not a measurement" (§6); no NAFEMS or other published target value is used, because "none is in the repository".
- **Critical path (ADR 0270 §4):** "0271 -> 0273 -> 0275 -> 0277 -> 0278 -> 0287 -> 0288", in seven waves of parallel packages in which no two packages edit the same file.
- **Advanced tier, not planned (ADR 0310 §1.3).** Group A, "no acceptance route in this repository": the fan's flutter margin, a validated life, creep growth or damped amplitude of a real blade, fibre orientation of a moulded blade, fatigue by code or guideline ("The documents are paid standards; house rule 4 forbids working from them"), the housing's sound level, bearing coefficients, the unsteady flag cases FSI2 and FSI3, progressive damage, mistuned discs. Group B, a route exists but left out by the cap of twenty-nine: explicit dynamics and frictional contact first (decision A6), then topology optimisation, a multigrid eigensolver, wedges and pyramids, a mixed displacement-pressure element, response spectra, heat conduction in the solid, several interfaces, the transport of the protocol, adaptation by the error estimate, mortar ties, cyclic symmetry with Coriolis, large rotations of beams and shells, structure-acoustic coupling, MPI transport.

## Open questions

- ADR 0290 N1 to N9 (N1 J2 plasticity moved to the advanced tier, N2 the CLI and MCP golden edits, N3 the `[solid]` table and its refusal, N4 no coupling crate, N5 rotating modes without the Coriolis term, N6 which shell, N7 reference values, N8 the scaffold's edits of basic-tier files, N9 roadmap placement): no decision record at the snapshot. The later registrations followed several recommendations (for example the triangular shell and the stress stiffening of shells are advanced items F-419 and F-420, as N6 (a) proposes).
- ADR 0310 A1 to A10, notably A1 (material data of the blade: "with (a) no life or creep number of the tier may be quoted as a prediction"), A2 (who fetches the flag benchmark's values for ADR 0337), A4 (the sparse factorisation stays opt-in), A6 (the group B cuts) and A8 (the fan's flutter margin not planned).
- ADR 0270 §6, "What I am unsure of": the reach of the direct solver, the hexahedron node order from the finite-volume mesh, and whether the tetrahedral mesher gives a usable mesh of a thin twisted blade.
