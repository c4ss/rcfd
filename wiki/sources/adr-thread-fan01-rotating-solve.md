# ADR thread: FAN-01 rotating solve (F-126, frozen rotor / MRF, rotor-stator interfaces)

**Origin:** the ADRs in the table below, `docs/adr/` at `origin/main` 2e94e5ef.
**Kind:** ADR thread. Created by the moving-meshes job (P3) as the home of the F-126 / FAN-01 chain; the wave ADRs of that chain (file names without an item, for example 0075, 0078, 0086, 0109, 0110, 0112, 0113, 0115, 0116, 0117, 0174 to 0181) belong to the records job, which adds them here. Until then this page holds only the item-level state and the links to the transient rotor-stator path.

F-126 is the hub item of the `fan` milestone in this category: "Turbomachinery rotating interfaces: frozen-rotor and stage (mixing-plane) interfaces with sector periodicity" ([[doc-roadmap-json]]). Its steady path is the rotating frame ([[mrf]]) on the FAN-01 blade passage ([[fan-01]], [[fan-passage-mesh]]); its transient alternative is the sliding interface ([[adr-thread-sliding-mesh-rotor-stator]]).

## Current state

- **F-126 is partial** (roadmap gap at 2e94e5ef): "The acceptance (a public compressor case reproducing pressure ratio and efficiency within published scatter) is not attempted. Also missing: MRF on the bladed FAN-01 passage (blade wall refused by the revolution check), compressible mixed-out state, rothalpy, a downstream-velocity constraint, and transient and coupled solvers." ([[roadmap-moving-meshes-rotors-and-coupling]]).
- **Transient rotor-stator from a case file exists** for laminar constant-property piso / pimple with Euler: full annulus (F-303, ADR 0637) and sector (F-711, ADR 0646) on the cylindrical-interface generators, which carry no blade ([[adr-thread-sliding-mesh-rotor-stator]]). No ADR of the moving-meshes list runs a sliding interface on the bladed FAN-01 passage or with turbulence.
- **Defaults held for the fan path.** On 2026-11-18 the maintainer held several default changes with the same words, "waits until the fan path needs it": the coupled periodic sliding step (ADR 0641, F-721; ADR 0659, F-724), the bounded second-order immersed forcing (ADR 0254, F-364), the mesh-speed compressible step (ADR 0257, F-365) and the fractional-step coupled-face opt-ins (ADR 0691, F-775).
- **Wave history to 2026-10-26 (records job, ADRs 0008 to 0117).** Frozen-rotor MRF in the steady SIMPLE kernel with analytic checks only (ADR 0008); the frame transformation, interface diagnostics and a mixing-plane library (ADR 0055), the mixing plane wired into the steady loop (ADR 0059); the rotating-frame solver shown second order without a fan (ADR 0071) and its finest-mesh limits met one refinement further (ADR 0075 part C). The F-126 acceptance attempt on FAN-01 (ADR 0075) "converged and MISSED every limit by a wide margin" (torque -38 %). The diagnosis then ran in four strands: where the deficit comes from (ADRs 0078, 0082, 0085: the momentum convection manufactures swirl on the rigid part of the relative velocity), how large the numerical error is (ADR 0086, the first solution verification: bands "not shown credible on this family"), why the steady run never converges (ADRs 0110, 0111, 0115: a limit cycle of the Barth-Jespersen-limited `linear_upwind`, with no attributed mechanism), and what geometry an MRF zone can take (ADRs 0109, 0112, 0113, 0116, 0117: cylindrical interface, cyclic seams, exact revolution sector).
- **Why MRF still cannot run on the bladed FAN-01 passage:** "The code shows that the blade blocks MRF whatever the hub and shroud are, and no generator removes this blocker"; `MrfZone::worst_non_axisymmetry` "still refuses the blade wall" (ADR 0116). The two absolute-velocity rotor-passage attempts F-RP (ADRs 0082, 0085) were removed, and "by user decision no third F-RP (blade-wall frame flux) attempt is drafted" (ADR 0116). Smoke and verification runs use the relative-frame `rotating_frame` source.
- **The stall has no attributed mechanism.** ADR 0110: on the bladed coarse mesh B3 "only first-order `upwind` (S10) STANDS", and "The ADR 0086 functionals are values taken on a cycle". ADR 0111: the frozen-limiter map's leading eigenvalue is "-1.3306 (real), and its mode sits at hub-region mid-chord cells, not at the leading edge"; the under-relaxed deferred correction F-R was removed; "The bladed stall has no attributed mechanism." ADR 0115: the range-scaled Venkatakrishnan limiter "does not stop the E3 cycle at any k". ADR 0117: the cyclic partner coefficient F1 "does not touch the limiter stall (`rho_du` 1.000)".
- **Part L is open:** the growth with refinement of the spurious outlet angular-momentum flux L on the empty passage "remains undiagnosed" (ADR 0110); in ADR 0117 F1 "CONTRIBUTES, not EXPLAINS THE GROWTH, so ADR 0110 part L stays open".
- **Opt-ins left by these waves** (default off or library-only, defaults bit-identical): `coupled_body_pair_mean` (steady, switched on with the rotating-frame wall treatment, ADR 0071; transient `PimpleSettings` variant without a schema key, ADR 0080); `wall_velocity_projection = "tangential"` and the C-grid construction `smoothed` (ADR 0072); `k_omega_sst` with a `moving_wall` and `boundaries.<p>.radial_frame_rate` (ADR 0078); `camber_seam` (ADR 0079) and `camber_seam_h` (ADR 0083); `solver.linear_upwind_limiter = "barth_jespersen"` (ADR 0081); `solver.velocity_correction = "face_flux"` (ADR 0084); the diagnostic hook `SimpleSolver::frozen_limiter` (ADR 0110); the 70 deg interface pre-flight, an interface sizing helper and the generator `annulus_3d_cylindrical_interface` (ADR 0112); `sources.<name>.cyclic_seams` with the solver flag `coupled_offdiag_in_h1` (ADR 0113); `Limiter::VenkatakrishnanRange(k)` with no case key (ADR 0115); the generator `annulus_3d_sector` (ADR 0116); `SimpleSolver::cyclic_partner_in_h1_test_only` with no case key (ADR 0117). See [[doc-case-schema-linear-upwind-limiter]].
- **Torques restated by ADR 0170 (2026-10-30):** the torques of ADRs 0075 and 0086 are molecular moments; with the effective moment default ADR 0075 attempt 3 gives "1.3471 / 1.0420 N m (-37.1 % / -44.6 %); F-126 still misses every limit", and the ADR 0086 bladed torques are "2.098164 / 2.028692 / 1.918189 N m, p 1.39, f_ext 2.2114, band +-0.2119 N m".
- Related items in the area that are not started: F-138 (harmonic balance and transient blade row); F-218 (actuator disk, partial) ([[roadmap-moving-meshes-rotors-and-coupling]]).

## Key numbers and limits

- **F-126 acceptance as registered on FAN-01 (ADR 0075):** "at each operating point `|Delta p_ts - measured| <= 3 Pa + 0.03 measured`, `|M/M_measured - 1| <= 0.05`, `|eta_ts - measured| <= 0.03`. MET only if both points meet all three." The scatter is the round robin of Vourakis and Karlsson 2022 (+-3 Pa, about 3 %). ## Wave 49: locating the bladed stall, and the EXP-ROT and M-FAN-B registrations (ADRs 0174 to 0184; added by job p3-records-b)

After ADR 0171 traced the NACA 0012 trailing-edge divergence to unlimited `linear_upwind` on sliver wall cells ([[adr-thread-naca0012-sa-drag]]), seven jobs (49K to 49R, 2026-10-30 to 2026-10-31) went after the Barth-Jespersen limit cycle of the bladed passage B3 (F3, 4 920 cells) and the held-out B2. They found where the cycle is driven but no fix: every candidate was "not accepted", and the last two jobs were stopped by their reading rule 6. ADR 0170 restated the FAN-01 torques under the effective moment (see Current state above). Then two milestone registrations of 2026-11-04 fixed the next experiments: EXP-ROT (F-276) and M-FAN-B (surface import, F-280).

- **The stall is not the trailing-edge mechanism** (ADR 0174, 49K): "By K2 and K3, FALSIFIED as predicted, the Barth-Jespersen stall is not the ADR 0171 mechanism." The largest-metric cell of E3 fails first under unlimited `linear_upwind`, "but that cell's metric is 750 and no FAN-01 cell reaches the registered 3 000"; SV302 is a false negative on FAN-01 "and by the maintainer's decision its level stays at 3 000". Question 2 recommended the scheme side.
- **The drive is the convection deferred correction, at two sites** (ADR 0175, 49L): the S10 stabilisation "comes from the convection deferred correction, not from the Laplacian"; by amendment 2 "the leading edge sustains a cycle and the hub does not". As an observation, "the leading-edge root is sufficient to drive a cycle but not necessary: without it, a non-local cycle takes over at the hub".
- **F-R holds the leading edge off, a hub cycle grows** (ADR 0177, 49N): F-R(0.25) "is not accepted: it misses Z2a, Z2b and Z2c (`rho_med` 1.038, `rho_du` 1.929, tolerance never reached)"; the leading-edge cycle "is gone within 10 iterations", torque and Delta p_ts stay in their bands, "but a larger cycle grows at hub cells at r 0.124-0.125, z 0.170-0.177". F-R(0.1) "is worse than the stall". The test-only field was kept.
- **The hub is the second autonomous driver** (ADR 0178, 49O): "the hub is necessary and sufficient". Removing the convection correction in ring H3 "(322 cells, 6.5 % of the mesh) stops the stall, with `is_converged()` true, torque 1.9144 N m and Delta p_ts 45.75 Pa. This is not a fix: the 322 cells are hand-picked".
- **No automatic fallback, no bounded scheme, no mesh change converges it.** ADR 0179 (49P): no theta in {0.3, 0.1, 0.03} meets C1, C2, C3 and S on B3; on B2 the mask "makes the run worse than its stall (`res_u` rises from 1.0e-3 to 7.8e-3)"; R1 missed (Delta_B3 0.0043, Delta_B2 0.0288). ADR 0180 (49Q): minmod, van Albada and van Leer all miss C1 to C3 on B3 and B2; "On B2 all three schemes cycle at the same five hub cells". ADR 0181 (49R): the site "is the hub trailing edge, not mid-chord ... what singles it out is the flow, a near-stagnant corner pocket"; M-R misses C1, C2, C3 and SB on both meshes; "The evidence points at the flow at the site rather than at a mesh defect, but this is a reading, not a proof".
- **What the 2026-11-04 loop instruction took** (ADRs 0180, 0181): "a limited scheme of the baseline's class is acceptable, and torque and Delta p_ts stay the ADR 0086 cycle means with a band until a converged second-order state exists"; the next FAN-01 stall job "is a report-only check of whether a steady state exists at the pocket"; `mesh.fan.c_grid_grading` stays "marked experimental" and was made hash-neutral (addenda of 2026-11-05).
- **EXP-ROT** (ADR 0183, F-276): arm 1 asks whether rigid rotation of the mesh removes the spurious outlet angular momentum of the empty passage (Q-ROT, the open part L), arm 2 whether the limit cycle stays when the rotation term is treated differently (Q-LC), check H whether the pocket behind the hub trailing edge "is physically unsteady" (Q-POCKET).
