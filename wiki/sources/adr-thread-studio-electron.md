# ADR thread: the Electron Studio, from the performance spike to the roadmap and its addenda (F-447, F-448, F-449, F-456, F-800 to F-838, F-852, F-888, F-890 to F-893)

**Origin:** the ADRs in the table below, `docs/adr/` at `origin/main` 709e302e (first written at 2e94e5ef; ADR 0786 added at 930153a4; ADRs 0781, 0787, 0788 and 0789 added at 709e302e, each read with its lead's reading; ADR 0790 added at 2514444c; ADRs 0350, 0353, 0800 and 0801 unchanged since 2e94e5ef).
**Kind:** ADR thread: one measurement spike with a decision rule (ADR 0350), its follow-up registration (ADR 0353), the roadmap ADR of the Electron Studio (ADR 0800) and the dependencies ADR that carries the per-item addenda (ADR 0801, 25 addenda); 4 ADRs, 2026-11-12 to 2026-11-17. ADRs 0350, 0800 and 0801 are in the list of job p3-records-b, which may add to this page; ADR 0353 is this job's.

On 2026-11-12 the maintainer asked for "an ADR to test the performance of electron<>rcfd APIs and potentially retiring rcfd-studio", after the lead recommended a web-technology shell for designer freedom. ADR 0350 registered the checks P1 to P12 and a decision rule before any code. On 2026-11-16 ADR 0800 turned the one item F-448 ("Electron Studio to parity") into F-800 to F-832, generated from three authorities (guardrails, `PRODUCT.md`, `DESIGN.md`), and the maintainer gave the go-ahead. ADR 0801 then justified the 14 npm dependencies and the layout of `apps/studio-web/`, and became the place where each Studio item records what it built.

## Current state

- **The spike** (ADR 0350, F-447 partial): both bulk routes are "fast enough and exact": R1 (the opt-in `data.*` methods, `rcfd-data/1`, service protocol 1.2) and R2 (a Node reader of `store.rts`); P3 (`monitors.get` of 4 series of 1e5 samples) missed at 127 ms against 100 ms. The rule "has no outcome for this case; the decision goes to the maintainer"; viability "is the maintainer's call (questions QA1 to QA5 of addendum 1)" per the roadmap gap. P3 was met after F-456 (ADR 0353, median 82.6 ms). Electron was chosen over Tauri (Q5 (a), taken by the lead on the standing instruction of 2026-11-11).
- **The roadmap** (ADR 0800, accepted 2026-11-16): F-800 to F-832 (later F-833 to F-838, F-848, F-852), each tracing to guardrails and to `PRODUCT.md` / `DESIGN.md`. The maintainer said yes to F-448 (Q1), keep `apps/studio-prototype/` until F-448 passes (Q2), Studio npm tests in CI as a job of `.github/workflows/ci.yml` (Q3). Two changes of 2026-11-16: no WCAG contrast requirement; guardrail G22 "Implicit by default, explicit on exception" with a word budget per surface.
- **The egui apps are gone** (ADR 0800 §10): the maintainer chose to "kill the old rcfd-studio and clear up some test bandwidth": `crates/rcfd-studio` and `crates/rcfd-gui` deleted on 2026-11-16 (F-449 done ahead of F-448). F-838 (2026-11-17): the local-model assistant `crates/rcfd-assistant` and the wgpu render core `crates/rcfd-render` removed, then `crates/rcfd-claude`; "Cargo.lock: 216 to 83 packages", then "83 to 82 packages".
- **Dependencies** (ADR 0801, accepted 2026-11-16): fourteen direct packages, six shipped (`react` 19.3.1, `react-dom` 19.3.1, `three` 0.187.0, `@react-three/fiber` 9.8.2, `react-aria-components` 1.22.1, `@fontsource/archivo` 5.3.0) and eight for building (`electron` 45.3.3, `vite` 8.3.8, `@vitejs/plugin-react` 6.3.0, `typescript` 7.0.2, `@types/*`), exact pins, lock file committed (119 packages), every licence AGPL-3.0 compatible.
- **Claude Code** (ADR 0801 Addendum 23, 2026-11-17): the maintainer asked to "completely remove the custom claude code panel and find a way to register the mcp in claude code itself and it should update the project in electron in realtime as it calls the mcp". The embedded panel, its allow-list writing and the Rust allow-list twin were removed; the Studio registers `claude mcp add rcfd-project --scope local -- "<engine rcfd-mcp>" --project "<folder>"`, opens Claude Code in a terminal ("Open Claude Code here"), and shows one activity line from the change feed, telling Claude Code's changes from its own by `rcfd-mcp --client studio`. "This supersedes F-814's panel (the parts of Addenda 18 and 20 about it) and A20's writing of the allow-list file."
- **Engine found without setup** (Addendum 21): every launch finds `rcfd`, `rcfd-mcp` and `rcfd-runner` from one folder with nothing set (`--no-engine`, `--engine-dir`, the `RCFD_STUDIO_*` variables, else `<resources>/bin` or `target/release`); "Nothing is ever looked for on PATH". Attaching to another service is `--service <host:port>` or `RCFD_STUDIO_SERVICE`, loopback only today.
- **Runner closes with the Studio** (unnumbered addendum, 2026-11-17, amends G7): the Studio owns only a runner that came up while it had the project open; an idle owned runner is shut down on close; with active runs the close guard asks (Stop runs and close / Keep them running and close / Cancel, the default).
- **Performance bench** (ADR 0800 §9, F-817 partial): every limit met on the idle baseline of 2026-11-17 (P10 median 320 ms against 2500; P2 p99 0.64 ms against 20; P4 R2 13.8 / 20.6 ms against 30 / 60; P5 63.9 per s; P7 44.7 ms; P8 240 per s). Under full load P10 "ranged from 1.2 s to 40.3 s across four runs within 75 minutes ... Its cause was not diagnosed." The bench stays out of CI (Q3).
- Two Studio end-to-end test items on 2026-11-23, ~~neither with a registration on main at 09647baa~~ (both merged since: ADR 0786 at 930153a4, ADR 0781 at 709e302e, see the bullets below) ([[doc-roadmap-json]]): F-852 (the test "Close session cannot be undone" times out on Windows in CI) was issued by the brief `docs/adr/briefs/f852-studio-close-session-timeout.md` (ADR 0781); F-888 (the quick run by keyboard fails on the lead's Windows machine where the Studio window lacks the system's focus, `toBeFocused` receives "inactive" on the Run button, while it passes on CI) was entered on the maintainer's answer "Yes, one over" and issued by `docs/adr/briefs/f888-studio-quick-run-focus.md` (ADR 0786), because that test "holds the merges of F-861, F-852, F-451 and F-339". Its acceptance also counts the `rcfd serve` processes that the end-to-end tests leave running after `npm test` on Windows.
- **F-888 done** (ADR 0786, merged at 8119447a, pushed at 0898008a, status at 930153a4): the cause was in the test, not the window's focus. After a choice in the select, react-aria gives the focus back to the select one animation frame later, and the test's Tab was sent before that frame, from the body; "inactive" is Playwright's word for any element not focused. With frames delayed by 300 ms the unchanged test failed 5 of 5; the fix waits for the focus to return, and the tab order and focus ring checks are kept. A second test, the gallery's select in `run-bar-keyboard.spec.ts`, needs no engine and runs on CI; it passed on windows-latest and ubuntu-24.04 (run 45224116370). The maintainer answered Q1 with (a): CI stays as it is, and the Studio's engine tests (275 of 852 on windows-latest) run only in the lead's push runs. Proposed partial (rule 8), done by the lead's reading ([[doc-roadmap-json]]).
- **The three follow-ups of ADR 0786 were entered and issued at once** (2026-11-23): the maintainer answered "Yes, issue all three" and "Yes, three over", so F-890, F-891 and F-892 were entered "with the titles, dependencies and acceptance of the proposal file as written" (ADR 0786, the maintainer's answer on the follow-ups) and three agents ran beyond the limit of 4, "for these items alone" (ADRs 0787 to 0789). They were asked first because the push runs of F-861 "failed npm test on the lead's machine in each of three runs on Studio tests outside it, which holds the merges of F-861, F-852, F-451 and F-339" ([[doc-roadmap-json]]). Merged as one joint check (`push_main.py --base`; merges 998fc7f6, 3e86bfb8, 9ce7ee53) and pushed at eca03964: "In the push run `npm test` failed once, on `run-control.spec.ts:320` alone, and passed all 873 tests in the second run; no `rcfd` process was left after the run, and the committed mutations of the three items were caught."
- **F-890 partial** (ADR 0787, the long task when the workspace tab opens): the first draw into a 2D canvas (the field legend's bar, `src/renderer/field/Legend.tsx`) makes Chromium create the renderer's shared GPU context by a synchronous call on the main thread, which the GPU process answers only "when the task its main thread is running has ended". The mechanism was shown behind a held GPU task (one `longtask` of 3400 to 3474 ms, 3 of 3) and found at 19 to 84 ms in 9 traces of 9 of the Studio's own flow. Fix: `createCanvasContextWhenIdle` (`src/renderer/ui/canvasContext.ts`) makes the call once at the page's first idle moment, "no later than 500 ms after", before any WebGPU work; a new test `workspace-first-open.spec.ts` with a committed mutation holds it. `responsiveness.spec.ts:260` then passed 10 of 10 runs alone and 3 of 3 whole runs with the limits of F-832 unchanged. Partial because "the run with the task of 400 to 440 ms was not made again", so that this mechanism was the lead's task "is inferred, not recorded" (the machine was 0.79 to 1.00 busy throughout; the record needs it under 0.75 busy).
- **F-891 done** (ADR 0788, the runner-budgets test `f837.spec.ts:198`): both failures are "a feed entry of this window's own earlier write reaching the panel at a time the test does not control (the feed is asked every 400 ms)". The first (the alert not found) is the order of the test's steps (forced: 8 of 8 failed with the lead's error text). The second (two alerts) is in the Studio: the panel "took any entry as another writer's change" and counted values as typed when none was (forced: 8 of 8; unforced 5 of 31 by the roadmap note). Fix: `budgetsFeed.ts` (`feedStep`, `typedIn`): the panel reads the file on a feed entry, acts only on another hash, and calls values typed only when a field differs; the test types before the other writer writes and waits by `data-file-change`. 20 of 20 runs alone, 5 of 5 whole runs; five mutations caught, one committed.
- **F-892 done** (ADR 0789, the `rcfd serve` left after `npm test`): the path is in the Studio's quit. "A Studio that quits while the `rcfd serve` it has just started is not yet connected leaves it running: detached, with no living parent"; Playwright's `close()` then never settles, the test and the worker's teardown time out. Shown by records: 9 of 20 quits with the real engine left one, 6 of 6 with a stand-in. `project.spec.ts:245` takes this path "but was not itself caught". Fix (`spawn.ts`, `client.ts`): nothing is spawned once the quit has begun, the child is known from the spawn on and ended by the quit, a child never connected to is ended; the tests' global teardown (`tests/support/leftovers.ts`, `global-teardown.ts`) ends and prints what a run leaves. After it 0 of 26 quits left one and 0 processes after each of 5 whole runs, 2 with a test made to time out; 6 mutations caught, one committed.
- **F-893 done** (ADR 0790, entered on the maintainer's answer "Yes, issue it" of 2026-11-24; issued on Claude Opus 5.5 after the Fable usage limit): `run-control.spec.ts:320` lost a race. The run list's note of a request goes once the runner has acted on it, and the runner held the solver within about 50 ms, so when the feed brought the settled list before the test looked at the row, line 329 found no note. The orphaned `rcfd run` solver came from the queue test failing between the long run's start and its entry in the clean-up's list: "the runner's shutdown leaves its solvers and its kill does not end its child". The fix is in the tests only: :320 reads the note's words as they appeared and the acting from the panel, the clean-up ends every run of the project, and the global teardown ends an `rcfd run` under a test's project. The lead's push run passed all 880 tests at the first run ([[doc-studio-guardrails]]).
- **F-852 done** (ADR 0781, "Close session cannot be undone" times out on Windows): a lost event. The settings page learned of the session Redo makes only from the service's event `created`, "and it replaced its events listener whenever the session shown changed"; a batch already posted to the old subscription was discarded and the page stayed on "Session … is not on the service" while `undo.spec.ts:268` waited 30 s. Shown by the new `undo-session-events.spec.ts`, which delays the events by 300 ms in the main process and failed on the unfixed source at the registered line. Fix: "one subscription per connection and service instance" in `SettingsPage.tsx` (10 lines added, 4 removed). The new spec 30 of 30 and the unedited `undo.spec.ts` 30 of 30 under load; the reverted fix is caught by a committed mutation. Without the delay the time-out did not occur in 30 runs on the unfixed source, and the page snapshots of the two CI failures were not read. Merged with F-451 as one joint check (merges 4255f915, 01726464), pushed at 9c1d3817 on 2026-11-24.
- **The capability inventory follows the CLI** (the lead's reading of ADRs 0781 and 0783): the first joint run of F-852 and F-451 failed `npm test` three times on `capabilities.spec.ts:210`, because `rcfd duct-modes` "was not yet in the Studio's capability inventory"; the lead regenerated it (`npm run capabilities`) and mapped the command in `coverage.json` "as the other acoustics tools (F-809, unplanned)", in the merge commit ([[doc-studio-coverage]], [[adr-thread-acoustic-wave-solver]]).

## Key numbers and limits

- **G9 limits** (ADR 0350, carried into the guardrails): P2 p99 <= 20 ms; P4 p50 <= 30, p99 <= 60 ms; P5 >= 10 snapshots per s, no frame > 150 ms; P7 median <= 1.5 s; P8 >= 60 frames per s, p99 frame <= 25 ms; P10 median <= 2.5 s.
- **Spike results** (ADR 0350 addendum 1, Electron 45.1.0, RTX 5080, 60 Hz display, load 0.3 to 0.6): R1 loads a 1e6-cell scalar to a GPU buffer "in p50 25.9 ms, p99 57.7 ms; R2 ... in p50 14.2 ms, p99 22.4 ms; JSON (R0) takes 552 ms"; "0 differing bits in 672e6 values"; cold start median 0.38 s; "R3 (WebAssembly) not run: it needs `unsafe` exports or `wasm-bindgen`".
- **F-456** (ADR 0353): replies equal to the old code byte for byte, "0 differing of 3000 stores (E1) and of 594 requests through the call and through the wire (E2, E3)"; the control on the unchanged server took 151.4 ms. I2 was later judged on "an increment of 100 samples per series of a store that grew by pushes, with the reallocation outside the timer, limit 2 ms unchanged" (maintainer, 2026-11-20); the old I2 is an ignored record.
- **Large meshes** (ADR 0800 §9, F-819 and F-819b): at 4e6 cells the scrub went from "11.3 to 14.8 per s, longest frame 83 to 101 ms" to "20.1 to 20.2 per s, longest frame 51 to 66 ms" after the viewport took surface values (1.22 MiB instead of 15.3 MiB); "No polyhedral FAN-01 mesh exists".
- **Pin policy** (ADR 0801 §4): a new direct dependency or major needs an ADR entry; Electron supports its latest three stable majors, "so 45 stays supported until about the release of 48".
- **Claude Code registration** (Addendum 23): checked against "the real CLI (Claude Code 2.1.326) in a scratch folder"; `get` "takes about 1.6 s because it starts the server to check it"; the project home's feed poll is 400 ms.
- **F-890** (ADR 0787, the lead's machine, AMD Ryzen 7 9800X3D, 0.89 to 1.00 busy in every record): in the held state the task was "3400, 3456, 3474 ms" before the fix and "66 ms, 56 ms, none over 50 ms" after it (limit 100 ms); in the ten runs alone the largest task of `workspace.tab` was 0 to 61 ms and the largest held frame 97 to 141 ms (limit 150); the WebGPU tasks the wait falls behind were "86 to 245 ms each, about 0.9 s of the first second after the mount". The size registered was 150000 bytes of reading, the actual "about 600000".
- **F-891** (ADR 0788): the window in which the first failure can fall is 41 to 58 ms against a poll of 400 ms, which "gives 10 % to 15 % for a run whose phase is not fixed"; it did not show without forcing (0 of 30 runs alone). The conflict note came 27 to 47 ms after the main process received the undo's entry.
- **F-892** (ADR 0789): of the 9 processes left in 20 quits, `service.json` was absent in 5 (spawned, not yet listening) and present in 4 (listening, not yet connected); the processes left were started with `--ui --journal-dir <tmp>/rcfd-studio-state-…/journals`. The teardown "printed no line in any run: nothing was left for it to end", so its sweep is shown only by a unit test.
- **F-893** (ADR 0790): the note was gone 26 to 96 ms after it appeared; :320 passed 15 of 15 runs of its file; whole runs gave 850/2/28, 850/2/28, 844/3/33 and 845/3/32 (passed, failed, did not run; the last two with a run-control test made to fail or time out), each leaving no `rcfd run`; 5 mutations caught, one committed.
- **F-852** (ADR 0781): the delay of the new spec is 300 ms; `undo.spec.ts` ran 5.6 to 7.7 s a run in the 30 runs under load (60 to 90 %). The whole `npm test` of that branch: 853 tests, 812 passed, 4 failed, 37 did not run. The whole run at the pushes of 2026-11-23 and 2026-11-24 has 873 tests.
