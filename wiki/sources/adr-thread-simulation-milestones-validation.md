# ADR thread: simulation milestones and their validation headlines (F-865, F-867, F-868, F-869, F-871, F-875, F-881, F-884, F-887)

**Origin:** the ADRs in the table below, `docs/adr/` at `origin/main` 09647baa (first written at 5b96a1f8).
**Kind:** ADR thread (registrations with result addenda, the lead's readings and the maintainer's answers), 11 ADRs, 2026-11-21 to 2026-11-23.

How the roadmap came to measure "per cent complete" per named simulation (F-865, ADR 0756), how the fan milestone was widened to the whole design goal (F-867, ADR 0757), and the first two registered comparisons that the maintainer's answers to ADR 0756 created as headline items: the NACA 0012 lift curve against Ladson (F-868, ADRs 0760 and 0764) and the mean drag of the cylinder at Re 100 (F-869, ADRs 0761 and 0763). The third headline created the same day, the FAN-01 design point (F-870, ADR 0762), has its own page: [[adr-thread-fan01-design-point]]. Added at 09647baa: the two diagnoses that the answers of 2026-11-22 created (F-884 by ADR 0770 with its follow-up F-887 by ADR 0772 on the NACA 0012; F-881 by ADR 0771 on the cylinder), the six further milestones (F-871, ADR 0775) and the milestone for the structure and vibration of a fan rotor (F-875, ADR 0779).
