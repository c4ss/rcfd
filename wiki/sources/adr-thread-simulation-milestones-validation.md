# ADR thread: simulation milestones and their validation headlines (F-865, F-867, F-868, F-869)

**Origin:** the ADRs in the table below, `docs/adr/` at `origin/main` 5b96a1f8.
**Kind:** ADR thread (registrations with result addenda, the lead's readings and the maintainer's answers), 6 ADRs, 2026-11-21 to 2026-11-22.

How the roadmap came to measure "per cent complete" per named simulation (F-865, ADR 0756), how the fan milestone was widened to the whole design goal (F-867, ADR 0757), and the first two registered comparisons that the maintainer's answers to ADR 0756 created as headline items: the NACA 0012 lift curve against Ladson (F-868, ADRs 0760 and 0764) and the mean drag of the cylinder at Re 100 (F-869, ADRs 0761 and 0763). The third headline created the same day, the FAN-01 design point (F-870, ADR 0762), has its own page: [[adr-thread-fan01-design-point]].

## Current state

- **F-868 done** (ADR 0764, maintainer's answer of 2026-11-22, option (a), "Yes, all six"): "a steady state reached by a time-accurate run of the same model on the same mesh meets the acceptance of F-868 at 17 degrees". At 17 degrees Spalart-Allmaras without DES on `pimple` (BDF2, dt 0.02 c/U to t = 200, mesh M1w-far500) settles to a steady attached flow with mean $c_l$ 1.5516 over [100, 200], 0.0541 (3.4 %) under Ladson's 1.6057 and inside the unchanged band 0.0803, outside the measured scatter 0.018; nothing sheds (range 0.0012). The control at 15 degrees gives 1.49167 against the steady 1.4932034 (tolerance 0.018). The lead had written the item partial at the merge because the acceptance names "incompressible steady RANS" and the state was reached by a time-accurate run; the maintainer's answer made it done. The steady route's failure at 17 degrees is the new item **F-884** (todo, milestone `aerofoil-lift`); the known miss of ADR 0760 stays as the record of the steady arm.
- **F-869 done** (ADR 0763, maintainer's answer of 2026-11-22, "Yes, option (c)"): "F-869 is done as it stands. The fine value, 1.33900, is in the band [1.314, 1.370], and the Richardson value is in the band for every order from 1 to 2.5 (1.343 to 1.354)"; the miss of W3 (b) (observed order 1.387 against [1.5, 2.5]) is kept as a known-miss test and a record, and the registered rule is not changed. New item **F-881** (todo, milestone `vortex-shedding`) diagnoses why the mean drag converges at an order near 1.4.
- **Milestone headlines at 5b96a1f8** ([[doc-roadmap-overview]], generated block): vortex shedding 20 items, 16 done, 3 partly done, 1 not started, 88 %, **headline met** (F-029 and F-869 met); aerofoil lift 23 items, 18 done, 4 partly, 1 not started, 87 %, headline not met (F-868 met, F-043 partly met); fan 92 items, 70 %, not met (F-870 partly met; see [[adr-thread-fan01-design-point]]).
- **Milestone figures exist and are generated.** F-865 is done (ADR 0756): `docs/milestones.json` defines the milestones, memberships are written into `docs/roadmap.json` (61 memberships of 32 items at the merge) and a block of `docs/roadmap-overview.md` is generated. The three per cents at the merge were 92, 89 and 90; the lead wrote that they "are high because the members of a milestone are ... what its run of record uses", so "The column 'Headline result' is the one to read" (ADR 0756, the lead's reading).
- **The fan milestone is the whole design goal** (F-867 done, ADR 0757): 89 members at the merge, headline from 2026-11-21: F-870, F-872, F-873, F-260, F-702 and F-874 (ADR 0757, answers of the maintainer).
- **Headline items from the maintainer's answers of 2026-11-21 to ADR 0756** (questions 1 to 3, option (b) each): F-868 (NACA 0012 lift curve), F-869 (cylinder mean drag), F-870 (FAN-01 design point). The six further milestones of question 4 are approved and are item F-871 (todo). ~~With F-869 entered, the vortex-shedding headline "reads 'partly met' from this day, where it read 'met' on the Strouhal number alone" (ADR 0756).~~ It reads "met" again since F-869 was made done on 2026-11-22 (ADR 0763, answers; [[doc-roadmap-overview]] at 5b96a1f8).
- The comparison of F-869 uses the option `time_scheme = Bdf2`; the default first-order step gives 1.30996 at the same mesh and step, outside the band. By the maintainer's answer to ADR 0761 question 2 (option (a)), F-869 is accepted with the option and "the default of the transient solver is not changed and no item is entered for it".

## Key numbers and limits

- **Milestone definitions** (ADR 0756, 0757): headline items per milestone in `docs/milestones.json`: F-029 and F-869 (vortex shedding); F-043 and F-868 (aerofoil lift); F-870, F-872, F-873, F-260, F-702 and F-874 (fan) ([[doc-milestones-json]]).
- **F-868 band** (ADR 0760): $\lvert c_l - c_{l,L}(\alpha)\rvert \le B(\alpha) = \max(0.05\,\lvert c_{l,L}(\alpha)\rvert,\, s),\ s = 0.018$. 0.05 is "the cl band of ADR 0167's LT10 and LT15"; s is the scatter of the three grit zones. Angles compared: 0, 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 13, 14, 15, 16 and 17 degrees; 18, 20, 22 and 24 are named as not compared. "A value that is not a flow value is a miss" (exit code 0, or ADR 0167's D5: window range of `cl_eff` <= 0.005 and of `cd_eff` <= 0.0005).
- **F-868 result table** (ADR 0760 addendum 1): V00 -0.0000 against -0.0072; V02 0.2202 against 0.2071; V10 1.0656 against 1.0586; V14 1.4235 and V15 1.4932 at the 8000-iteration cap (exit code 5) but flow values; V16 1.5420 against 1.5563 (difference -0.0143, band 0.0778); V17 1.0476 iteration mean against 1.6057, band 0.0803, "over its last 1000 iterations cl swings between -0.066 and 1.955". Twelve runs, 46 minutes in all; binary sha256 `00848da110de638130b0dfa2c9af197b04fa34039edf9b7234222aec2ec8b081`.
- **F-868 band for later registrations:** the lead decided ADR 0760 question 2 (his to decide): "a later registration against this measured set takes the scatter of the three grit zones, 0.018, as the band below stall, and not the larger of it and 5 per cent." The lead notes that "from 6 degrees up the 5 per cent is 3.5 to 125 times the difference found".
- **F-869 band** (ADR 0761): "B = [1.314, 1.370]: the smallest and the largest of the eleven published values ... without the row '(Present 32 x 16)'". Width 0.056, middle 1.342, half-width 2.09 %. K2's window of ADR 0060, "within 4 % of 1.342", [1.2883, 1.3957], is kept for the coarse mesh only.
- **F-869 first sequence** (ADR 0761, BDF2, far field 20 D, dt 0.05): 64 x 50 1.31674; 90 x 70 1.33879; 128 x 100 1.35247 (V1, St 0.16480, equal to ADR 0069); 128 x 100 at dt 0.025 1.34511; at far field 30 D 1.33884; Richardson value 1.37479, "0.0048 above the upper end". Arithmetic, not a check: halving dt lowers the drag by 0.00736; the far field at 20 D raises it by 0.0136 against 30 D.
- **F-869 second sequence** (ADR 0763, BDF2, dt 0.025, far field 30 D, last 8 whole periods): 90 x 70 1.32259 (St 0.16169); 128 x 100 1.33285 (St 0.16395); 181 x 141 1.33900 (St 0.16486). Ratios 1.42539 and 1.41203; observed order 1.387 against [1.5, 2.5]. Formula values printed "as registered and not results": 1.34901 at the observed order, 1.34519 at order 2; GCI_fine 0.935 %. "It reaches the upper end 1.370 only for an order under 0.52."
- **F-869 reading of the order** (ADR 0763): the order "is the same as on the sequence of ADR 0761 (1.38 at dt 0.05 and 20 D...)", so "the expectation of the registration, that the time error held the order down, is therefore not supported: the low order looks like a property of the spatial discretisation". Candidates named and not examined: "the strongly graded O-grid, the wall force integral, the far-field condition".

- **ADR 0764 set-up:** Spalart-Allmaras without DES on `pimple`, `time_scheme = "bdf2"`, dt 0.02 c/U to t = 200 (10 000 steps, three outer iterations a step), mesh M1w-far500 (sha256 `59e707a8...3a70c889`, ADR 0167's), statistics over [100, 200]; runs at 17 degrees (U-a17) and 15 degrees (control U-a15), 11 064 s and 11 139 s side by side. The fallback (dt 0.01) was not needed.
- **ADR 0764 checks:** U17 $\lvert M(17) - 1.6057\rvert \le 0.0803$, measured 1.5516 (difference -0.0541, margin 1.5); S17 settled $\lvert M_2 - M_1\rvert \le 0.018$, measured -0.0003; C15 $\lvert M(15) - 1.4932034\rvert \le 0.018$, measured 1.49167 (-0.0015); S15 +0.0005; FT17 (50 steps on the coarse mesh) R17 = 1.2079835934; NC1 0.558 against 0.0803; NC2 +0.0682 and -0.0504 against 0.018. Starting-vortex estimate before the run: $\delta c_l = c_l\,c/(2Ut)$, 0.35 % of $c_l$ over [100, 200]. Drag reported without a claim: $c_d$ 0.02727 at 15 degrees and 0.04369 at 17 degrees.
- **ADR 0764 reading of the steady arm:** "the state exists and the steady iteration did not find it. That is a finding about the steady iteration at this angle; its cause was not examined." The computed maximum over the compared angles becomes 1.5516 at 17 degrees, 3.7 % under the measured 1.6116 at 17.13. Held by `crates/rcfd-api/tests/naca0012_17_degrees_unsteady_f868.rs` (12 tests, 7 s); four mutations caught.
- **F-884 acceptance** (roadmap at 5b96a1f8): a steady solve at 17 degrees on M1w-far500 "ends steady by ADR 0167's window rule with a lift coefficient within 0.018 of 1.5516 ... by a registered change of the steady route that leaves every default bit-identical, or the cause of the non-convergence of the SIMPLEC iteration there is diagnosed".
- **F-881 acceptance** (roadmap at 5b96a1f8): the observed order (1.38 on ADR 0761's sequence, 1.387 on ADR 0763's) "is explained by a cause that a registered run shows: candidates named before the run (among them the grading of the O-grid, the wall force integral, the far-field condition), each tested by a run that changes it alone"; no solver change by the item.
