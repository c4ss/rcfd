# ADR thread: FAN-01 at its design point (F-870, F-878, F-879, F-882, F-883, F-885)

**Origin:** the ADRs in the table below, `docs/adr/` at `origin/main` 09647baa (first written at 5b96a1f8).
**Kind:** ADR thread, 6 registrations (ADR 0762 for F-870; its follow-ups ADR 0765 for F-878 and ADR 0766 for F-879; then ADR 0767 for F-885, ADR 0768 for F-882 and ADR 0769 for F-883), each with a result addendum and the lead's reading, most with the maintainer's answers (2026-11-22), framed by ADRs 0756 and 0757.

F-870 is the headline item of the `fan` milestone that asks for the FAN-01 pressure rise and shaft power at the design point within a band built from "the measurement's stated uncertainty and a mesh-convergence estimate". It was created by the maintainer's answer to ADR 0756 question 3 (option (b), 2026-11-21) and made part of the whole fan design goal by ADR 0757. ADR 0762 is the first registration: it tabulates the state of every earlier FAN-01 diagnosis (ADRs 0075 to 0183), registers the band, runs mesh B1 once and ranks the gap. ADR 0765 (F-878) compares the solve with the measured LDA profiles behind the rotor and tries to divide the missing pressure rise between rotor and installation; ADR 0766 (F-879) runs check H of ADR 0183, the hub trailing-edge pocket in time. The rotating-frame solve and its stall diagnoses themselves are the subject of [[adr-thread-fan01-rotating-solve]] (job p3-moving-meshes), if that page exists; this page holds the design-point ADR. The three follow-ups merged on 2026-11-22 and 2026-11-23 each answer one of the maintainer's answers below: ADR 0769 (F-883) reads a measured static pressure behind the rotor from the record's wall-pressure data, ADR 0768 (F-882) divides the simulated loss of total pressure and recomputes it on a finer mesh, and ADR 0767 (F-885) adds the record of the outer iterations and runs check H again by ADR 0183's own rule.

## Key numbers and limits

- **Measurement** (ADR 0762): row 13 of `w34d_fan01_characteristic_n1ug.csv`, 1.40324 m^3/s, 126.45658 Pa, 333.30090 W; speed 1486 rpm; shaft torque compared 2.14184 N m. The v6 record (doi:10.5281/zenodo.23943494) says "The bearing friction was measured with the fan removed and subtracted", which removes bearing loss from the list of unknowns of ADRs 0075, 0078 and 0086.
- **Band form:** met iff $\lvert f_{B1} - f_{measured}\rvert \le U_{num} + u_{meas}$, the two added, not combined in quadrature. $U_{num} = 1.25\,\lvert f_2 - f_1\rvert / (r_{21}^{p_{band}} - 1)$, $p_{band} = \min(p, 1)$, $r_{21} = (38184 / 13627)^{1/3} = 1.40981$. Torque triplet 2.098164 / 2.028692 / 1.918189 N m gives p 1.3928 and U_num 0.21190 N m (32.98 W); pressure triplet 73.988 / 64.233 / 46.778 Pa gives p 1.7378 and U_num 29.75 Pa. $u_{meas}$ "not registered". See [[gci-richardson]].
- **Mesh gates** (from `docs/meshing.md`, set-up A, and the gap of F-133): non-orthogonality <= 70 deg, skewness <= 2.0, aspect ratio <= 300, by `rcfd_mesh::quality::check_mesh`.
- **Convergence rule:** `Simulation::is_converged()` within 8000 iterations with `tolerance_u = tolerance_p = 1e-5`; "The cap rule of ADR 0075 and ADR 0086 is reported beside it and does **not** count as converged for F-870".
- **Best recorded values before the run** (task 1): torque B1 2.098164 N m effective (326.50 W, 2.0 % below), pressure 73.99 Pa; best pressure ever, arm KN of ADR 0078, 76.2 Pa on the H-slit with minmod (molecular torque 2.0163 N m). The only converged bladed state above first order is ADR 0178's B3 (1.9144 N m, 45.75 Pa) with first order in 322 hand-picked hub cells.
- **Shape of the miss** (the agent's inference, "no run of this work shows it", per the lead): the rotor raises total pressure by 157.25 Pa on B1 (ADR 0086), total-to-total efficiency 0.68; 157.25 - 73.99 = 83.3 Pa leaves as velocity (53.9 Pa axial dynamic pressure, 23.5 Pa swirl); the miss of 52.5 Pa is 63 % of it. ADR 0086's extrapolated pressure rise 85.9 Pa is still 40.5 Pa (32 %) below the measurement.
- **Ranked gap** (ADR 0762, task 4): 1 the compared quantity (rotor-only passage against an installation value; follow-ups LDA comparison, then outlet model); 2 no converged second-order solution (check H of ADR 0183 first); 3 the mesh gates (F-133); 4 resolution and credibility of the estimate; 5 the rotating-frame discretisation (0.106 N m of spurious outlet angular momentum in the empty passage, F-126); 6 the unknowns of the measurement; 7 geometry and model (CAD chords 4 to 5 % shorter, no root fillet, fully turbulent SST at chord Re 1.25e5 to 1.5e5).

Verdict "installation's" if $R_{hi} + U < D/2$, "rotor's" if $R_{lo} - U > D/2$, else "cannot be told". The interval is 93.56 Pa wide before any band, "wider than $D$".

"Every value the formula gives for an order from 1 to 2.786 is between 41.9 and 53.0 Pa, so $I_0$ between 35.2 and 46.3 Pa of the 52.47 Pa missing (ADR 0765), under the condition $L_m = L_0$; none of these is a result." Reported, not explained: the inlet-duct share is not monotone (7.3, 5.1, 3.1, 7.3 Pa). At $\delta$ 1.25 and 5 mm the blade part on B0c is 14.48 and 19.44 Pa. Mutation M6 as registered was not caught; an unregistered variant M6w was. See [[gci-richardson]].
- **ADR 0767, the record:** on the cavity (three steps of 5 outer iterations) the record and the second route agree to the 13 printed digits (step 1: 3.4197e-2, 7.1717e-3, 1.5369e-3, 3.3740e-4, 1.4550e-3 m/s); with the lid at rest every $d_k$ is exactly 0; with the setting off `outer_velocity` is empty and `u`, `p` and the flux are equal in bits. On the cavity $d_5$ is 4 to 15 times $d_4$.
- **ADR 0767, check H again** (B2, 5 498 and 10 996 steps, 156.8 and 256.8 min): window steps meeting $d_5 \le 10^{-2}\,d_1$: 0 and 0. $d_5/d_1$ median 0.9333 (H(dt)) and 0.9644 (H(dt/2)); $d_1$ median 1.0146 and 0.9735 m/s; medians of $d_2/d_1$, $d_3/d_2$, $d_4/d_3$, $d_5/d_4$: 0.209, 0.602, 1.014, 6.70 and 0.199, 0.662, 0.919, 19.4; the change of a whole step is 0.093 and 0.259 m/s. Three facts read from it: $d_1$ and $d_5$ "do not shrink as the state settles and do not scale with the step"; the step change is smaller than either; the relaxed iterations contract and the unrelaxed 5th jumps. "Together: the relaxed and the unrelaxed outer iterations do not share a fixed point, and each step oscillates between the two by about 1 m/s at some cell."
- **ADR 0767, the candidate cause** ("read from the code and not shown by a run"): the relaxation of the momentum predictor is implicit about the current iterate (`FvMatrix::relax`), "so it alone leaves the fixed point unchanged; but the relaxed diagonal also enters the correctors' `rAU` and the face interpolation built on it, which change when alpha goes to 1 in the final iteration. That is a candidate cause, not a diagnosis." Relaxed iterations use `alpha_u = 0.9`. "On this set-up ADR 0183's rule cannot be met by any state, steady or not." For F-870: the time means of ADR 0766 now rest "on a march whose outer iterations do not converge within a step, so the time means themselves carry an error of the outer loop of unknown size".
