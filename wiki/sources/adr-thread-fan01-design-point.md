# ADR thread: FAN-01 at its design point (F-870, F-878, F-879)

**Origin:** the ADRs in the table below, `docs/adr/` at `origin/main` 5b96a1f8.
**Kind:** ADR thread, 3 registrations (ADR 0762 for F-870 and its two follow-ups, ADR 0765 for F-878 and ADR 0766 for F-879), each with a result addendum, the lead's reading and the maintainer's answers (2026-11-22), framed by ADRs 0756 and 0757.

F-870 is the headline item of the `fan` milestone that asks for the FAN-01 pressure rise and shaft power at the design point within a band built from "the measurement's stated uncertainty and a mesh-convergence estimate". It was created by the maintainer's answer to ADR 0756 question 3 (option (b), 2026-11-21) and made part of the whole fan design goal by ADR 0757. ADR 0762 is the first registration: it tabulates the state of every earlier FAN-01 diagnosis (ADRs 0075 to 0183), registers the band, runs mesh B1 once and ranks the gap. ADR 0765 (F-878) compares the solve with the measured LDA profiles behind the rotor and tries to divide the missing pressure rise between rotor and installation; ADR 0766 (F-879) runs check H of ADR 0183, the hub trailing-edge pocket in time. The rotating-frame solve and its stall diagnoses themselves are the subject of [[adr-thread-fan01-rotating-solve]] (job p3-moving-meshes), if that page exists; this page holds the design-point ADR.

## Current state

- **Maintainer's answers of 2026-11-22 to ADRs 0764 to 0766** ("Yes, all six", each the agent's recommendation, which the lead shared) (ADR 0765 and ADR 0766, "Answers of the maintainer"):
- ADR 0765 Q1, option (b): the Euler head and the kinetic energy leaving the rotor over the measured span and the profiles of $c_x$ and $c_t$ at the measured plane become compared quantities of F-870 "beside the pressure rise and the shaft power, each with a band registered before its run; the pressure rise stays a compared quantity and its miss stays a miss". The acceptance of F-870 is extended by a sentence that says so (roadmap at 5b96a1f8). This answers ADR 0762 Q4 as to the compared quantity (ADR 0762, "Question 4 answered").
- ADR 0765 Q2, options (b) and (c): first the loss budget of the simulated rotor on a finer mesh, entered as **F-882**; beside it a short reading of the wall-pressure data of the record for a mean static pressure behind the rotor, entered as **F-883**. **F-880** (the outlet as on the rig) waits on both; F-882 and F-883 are added to its dependencies. Not issued.
- ADR 0766 Q1, option (a): an opt-in, read-only record of the velocity change of each outer iteration is added to the transient solver and check H is run again by ADR 0183's own outer-iteration rule, entered as **F-885**. "The existing records are not read as STEADY."
- ADR 0766 Q2: the convergence rule of F-870 is decided after F-885 reports, "with the agent's caution recorded: the time-marched pressure rise on B2 is 8 to 9 per cent above the SIMPLEC sample". This answers the convergence half of ADR 0762 Q4.
- ADR 0766 Q3, option (a): the acceptance of F-879 is reworded from "from the stalled steady state" to "from the start state registered there" (the uniform start, departure D-7).
- **F-879 partial** (ADR 0766, written partial as proposed): check H was run on B2 as H(dt) with 4.605e-5 s and H(dt/2), 0.2532 s or 6.27 revolutions each, and by the registered rule "the run does not decide": H(dt) meets the outer-iteration rule D-2 at 43.76 % of the window's steps against 99 % (H(dt/2): 100 %), so the class of ADR 0183 is NOT EVALUABLE. The agent's own reading: the substitute quantity of D-2 (a pressure ratio, because the solver keeps no velocity change of an outer iteration) "does not measure the time accuracy of this state". Observed, not a class: the site oscillation decays in both runs (window amplitude 0.0054 and 0.0065 m/s against the STEADY threshold 0.193 m/s; below 1e-4 m/s in the last flow-through time), with no reversed axial flow at the six site cells after the first flow-through time.
- **F-878 partial** (ADR 0765, written partial as proposed): the LDA record is traced (Zenodo v6, `lda_data.zip`, checksums in `docs/reference-data.d/0765-F-878.part`; [[doc-reference-data-f878-fan01-lda-profiles]]) and its datum settled (x = 90 mm from the upstream hub face). On B1 at x = 90 mm $c_x$ is within its band at 14 of 26 radii and $c_t$ at 14 of 26 (largest excess 3.54 and 1.36 m/s): **12 of 26 radii miss in each component**. Over the measured span the Euler work is 297.66 W measured and 312.03 W simulated (pointwise), the Euler head 220.02 against 226.86 Pa, the kinetic energy leaving the rotor 99.20 against 102.28 Pa. "Of the missing 52.47 Pa it cannot be told from the measured velocity profiles what part is the rotor's and what part the installation's": the rotor's share lies between -34.4 and +59.1 Pa, because "an LDA measures no pressure, so the loss of the measured rotor is not known". At equal rotor loss the rotor's share would be -3.8 Pa and the installation's 56.2 Pa, +-31.1 Pa: "the missing pressure is not in the work of the rotor or in its outflow velocity". The lead reads the simulated loss (104.34, 78.75, 62.87 Pa on B3, B2, B1, not converged) as making "the loss budget on a finer mesh the next step that can decide".
- **F-870 partial** (ADR 0762, result unchanged). By the ADR's own rule the work is "stopped and reported" on the band: "the stated uncertainty of the FAN-01 measurement is in no source that could be read". On B1 (38 184 cells, 8000 iterations): total-to-static pressure rise 73.988 Pa against 126.46 Pa (-41.5 %, a miss against the numerical band 29.75 Pa); shaft power 326.50 W against 333.30 W (-2.0 %, inside the numerical part 32.98 W, "not a pass of the clause"). Held by `crates/rcfd-api/tests/f870_fan01_design_point.rs` (three gate tests and a heavy record of B1).

## Key numbers and limits

- **Measurement** (ADR 0762): row 13 of `w34d_fan01_characteristic_n1ug.csv`, 1.40324 m^3/s, 126.45658 Pa, 333.30090 W; speed 1486 rpm; shaft torque compared 2.14184 N m. The v6 record (doi:10.5281/zenodo.23943494) says "The bearing friction was measured with the fan removed and subtracted", which removes bearing loss from the list of unknowns of ADRs 0075, 0078 and 0086.
- **Band form:** met iff $\lvert f_{B1} - f_{measured}\rvert \le U_{num} + u_{meas}$, the two added, not combined in quadrature. $U_{num} = 1.25\,\lvert f_2 - f_1\rvert / (r_{21}^{p_{band}} - 1)$, $p_{band} = \min(p, 1)$, $r_{21} = (38184 / 13627)^{1/3} = 1.40981$. Torque triplet 2.098164 / 2.028692 / 1.918189 N m gives p 1.3928 and U_num 0.21190 N m (32.98 W); pressure triplet 73.988 / 64.233 / 46.778 Pa gives p 1.7378 and U_num 29.75 Pa. $u_{meas}$ "not registered". See [[gci-richardson]].
- **Mesh gates** (from `docs/meshing.md`, set-up A, and the gap of F-133): non-orthogonality <= 70 deg, skewness <= 2.0, aspect ratio <= 300, by `rcfd_mesh::quality::check_mesh`.
- **Convergence rule:** `Simulation::is_converged()` within 8000 iterations with `tolerance_u = tolerance_p = 1e-5`; "The cap rule of ADR 0075 and ADR 0086 is reported beside it and does **not** count as converged for F-870".
- **Best recorded values before the run** (task 1): torque B1 2.098164 N m effective (326.50 W, 2.0 % below), pressure 73.99 Pa; best pressure ever, arm KN of ADR 0078, 76.2 Pa on the H-slit with minmod (molecular torque 2.0163 N m). The only converged bladed state above first order is ADR 0178's B3 (1.9144 N m, 45.75 Pa) with first order in 322 hand-picked hub cells.
- **Shape of the miss** (the agent's inference, "no run of this work shows it", per the lead): the rotor raises total pressure by 157.25 Pa on B1 (ADR 0086), total-to-total efficiency 0.68; 157.25 - 73.99 = 83.3 Pa leaves as velocity (53.9 Pa axial dynamic pressure, 23.5 Pa swirl); the miss of 52.5 Pa is 63 % of it. ADR 0086's extrapolated pressure rise 85.9 Pa is still 40.5 Pa (32 %) below the measurement.
- **Ranked gap** (ADR 0762, task 4): 1 the compared quantity (rotor-only passage against an installation value; follow-ups LDA comparison, then outlet model); 2 no converged second-order solution (check H of ADR 0183 first); 3 the mesh gates (F-133); 4 resolution and credibility of the estimate; 5 the rotating-frame discretisation (0.106 N m of spurious outlet angular momentum in the empty passage, F-126); 6 the unknowns of the measurement; 7 geometry and model (CAD chords 4 to 5 % shorter, no root fillet, fully turbulent SST at chord Re 1.25e5 to 1.5e5).

- **ADR 0765, the record** ([[doc-reference-data-f878-fan01-lda-profiles]]): two-component LDA, one operating point (1.4 m^3/s, 1486 per minute), planes x = 20 and x = 90 mm, 26 radii from $r/250\,\text{mm} = 0.532$ to 0.984 (133 to 246 mm, 90.88 % of the annulus area). No LDA uncertainty is stated, so the band "has no measurement part". Plane in the mesh: offset 45.15 mm +-1.3 mm, `z_mesh = 0.1 m + (x - 21.45 mm)`, the plane x = 90 at z = 0.16855 m.
- **ADR 0765, the division rule** (registered before the run): $\Delta p_{ts} = \Pi + Rec$, $\Pi = e - q - L$; rotor share $R = \Pi_m - \Pi_s$, installation share $I = D - R$, $D = 126.46 - 73.99 = 52.47$ Pa. Since $L_m \ge 0$ and $Rec_m \le q_m$: $R_{lo} = 126.46 - q_m - \Pi_s \le R \le R_{hi} = e_m - q_m - \Pi_s$. Verdict "installation's" if $R_{hi} + U < D/2$, "rotor's" if $R_{lo} - U > D/2$, else "cannot be told". The interval is 93.56 Pa wide before any band, "wider than $D$".
- **ADR 0765, results on B1** (B2, B3 beside): $\Pi_s$ 61.71 Pa (54.73, 39.35); loss $L_s$ 62.87 Pa (78.75, 104.34); $Rec_s$ 12.28 Pa; $R_{lo}$ -34.45 Pa (band 23.75), $R_{hi}$ 59.11 Pa (band 24.95), $R_{eq}$ -3.76 Pa (band 31.10, 27.17 of it the mesh term); $I_{eq}$ 56.23 Pa. Rig: $Rec_m - L_m = +5.64$ Pa. Whole-annulus Euler work on B1 331.62 W (profile) and 323.85 W (pointwise) against 326.50 W of torque times $\omega$. The limit cycle moves profile values by at most 0.0006 m/s. At x = 20 mm the measurement carries a swirl against the rotation (-36.22 / -33.55 W); the solve has none (+3.38 W).
- **ADR 0765, where the profiles miss:** between $r/250\,\text{mm}$ = 0.69 and 0.83 the solve is 0.8 to 1.1 m/s low in $c_x$; at the hub (0.532) 1.2 m/s high in $c_x$ and 1.6 m/s low in $c_t$; outside 0.97 it has no through-flow ($c_x$ -0.25 and -0.98 m/s where 3.9 and 3.6 m/s were measured). The B1 radial layers are coarser than the 5 mm measured spacing, so "the count of 14 is therefore a weak number in both directions". Held by `crates/rcfd-api/tests/f878_fan01_lda_profiles.rs` (4 gate tests, a heavy record, the known miss `f878_b1_profiles_acceptance_known_miss`); six mutations caught.
- **ADR 0766, check H as run:** B2 (13 627 cells), k-omega SST, `pimple` with 5 outer iterations, BDF2, limited `linear_upwind`, seam diffusivity `pair` (D-1), uniform start (D-7). $dt = T_c/100 = 4.605\times10^{-5}$ s and $2.3025\times10^{-5}$ s; 5 498 and 10 996 steps; the window is the last 0.1266 s (3.14 revolutions); wall time 131.3 and 230.2 min. STEADY threshold $A \le 0.01\,\Omega r_{hub} = 0.193$ m/s. Outer rule of ADR 0183: in at least 99 % of the window's steps the velocity change of the 5th outer iteration is at most 1e-2 of that of the 1st; D-2 read it on a pressure ratio.
- **ADR 0766, measured:** site amplitude 5.365e-3 m/s (H(dt)) and 6.493e-3 m/s (H(dt/2)), no upward crossings; window means 2.007096 and 2.010156 N m (312.33 and 312.81 W) and 69.6172 and 70.0729 Pa, against B2 SIMPLEC 2.028692 N m and 64.2329 Pa (ADR 0762) and the measured 2.14184 N m and 126.46 Pa. The time march has 1.0 % less torque and 8.4 to 9.1 % more pressure rise than the SIMPLEC sample of the same mesh. The largest unsteadiness left is at the tip (0.16 m/s in H(dt), 0.52 m/s in H(dt/2)). Held by `crates/rcfd-api/tests/f879_fan01_check_h.rs` (four gate tests, two heavy records); six of seven mutations caught.
