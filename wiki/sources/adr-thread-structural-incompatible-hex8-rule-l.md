# ADR thread: structural solver, the incompatible-modes Hex8 at finite strain, its stabilisation (rule L) and the 16^3 punch path

**Origin:** the ADRs in the table below, `docs/adr/` at `origin/main` 2e94e5ef.
**Kind:** ADR thread (registrations, diagnoses and measurements with result addenda), 28 ADRs, 2026-11-15 to 2026-11-17. Job p3-structural-b. The small-strain B-bar and incompatible-modes Hex8 (F-390, ADR 0493) are on [[adr-thread-structural-verification-elements]]; the F-bar Hex8 (ADR 0506) on [[adr-thread-structural-materials-nonlinear]].

The longest single chain of the structural category: the [[incompatible-modes-hex8]] taken to finite strain in the Newton driver of [[rcfd-fem]] (F-596), the discovery that it has a spurious zero (hourglass) eigenvalue in compression, an opt-in stabilisation whose diagnostic coefficient "rule L" became the reference rule, then a confined punch block at $\nu = 0.49$ that rule L could not solve on distorted and refined meshes. The chain diagnoses that failure down to a single element (3720) and a subcritical bifurcation of the global path, and ends with the local mode update (the warm start) measured as a candidate default.

## Current state

- **The element.** The finite-strain incompatible-modes Hex8 is done (F-596, [ADR 0608](../../docs/adr/0608-f596-registration-incompatible-modes-hex8-at-finite-strain.md)): the enhanced deformation gradient of Simo and Armero with the nine Wilson modes and Taylor's centre-Jacobian correction, the modes solved by a local Newton iteration per element evaluation and condensed in the Newton loop (module `rcfd_fem::nl_incompatible`).
- **Hourglass in compression.** Under homogeneous uniaxial stress on a distorted `6 x 6 x 6` patch the lowest eigenvalue of the condensed tangent reaches a spurious zero at $\lambda_s = 0.60797$ (F-685, ADR 0614, partial: MU7 not caught). An opt-in stabilisation `IncompatibleStabilisation` (Off by default) has three coefficients: `RestStress` (ADR 0614), `Deviatoric` (no bulk modulus) and `LinearStress` ("rule L", the bulk modulus linear through the stress) (ADR 0630). ADR 0630 shows that no coefficient without the bulk modulus can stabilise confined compression near incompressibility (`Deviatoric` misses S1D at $\nu = 0.45$ and `0.49`); rule L met every check in all six cases (F-703 partial).
- **Rule L under load.** On a confined punch block at $\nu = 0.49$ and a bending cantilever, rule L is within `0.121` (coarse) and `0.066` (refined) of the F-bar Hex8 punch reaction where the Full Hex8 is `0.34` to `0.79` off (F-708, ADR 0636, partial by MU24). On distorted meshes it fails on one of eight (the refined block at `0.25 h`, DB4; F-719, ADR 0649).
- **The punch-edge failure, diagnosed.** The cause of DB4 is an overshooting iterate of the local mode iteration from $\alpha = 0$, not the compatible or converged enhanced field (F-732, ADR 0655). Remedies measured: line search plus backtracking (remedy R) and a sufficient-decrease test S pass the eight meshes but fail two of three further seeds (F-739, ADR 0658); a **warm start** of the mode iteration from the committed modes (remedy W, `SolidModel::set_incompatible_mode_warm_start`) passes all fourteen set-ups, while a Levenberg-Marquardt trust region does not (F-740, ADR 0660). W also works with J2, on the transient and rotating paths (F-742, ADR 0666) and with creep and viscoelastic materials (F-748, ADR 0668, done).
- **J2 accuracy.** With J2, rule L is 17 and 12 per cent below F-bar on the node-set punch (ADR 0666); a three-mesh reading on $4^3$, $8^3$, $16^3$ found a common limit with F-bar carrying the larger error at the punch edge (F-747, ADR 0678). Under a smoothed pressure without the edge jump, rule L with J2 is within `2.6` and `0.5` per cent of F-bar on $8^3$ and $16^3$, and F-bar's edge excess disappears (F-762, ADR 0683).
- **The 16^3 neo-Hookean punch path.** Rule L loses positive definiteness at load factor `0.966` (ADR 0678, ADR 0682). The chain then shows: the cold local Newton jumps off an admissible branch; the equilibrium at `0.965625` exists and is unstable, one negative eigenvalue, an hourglass mode in element 3720 (F-774, ADR 0694); the inertia changes between `0.964899444580` and `0.964900207520`, a **bifurcation**, not a limit point (F-779, ADR 0697); it is a **subcritical pitchfork**, cubic coefficient `-1.16e12` (F-783, ADR 0700); the branch traced toward lower loads ends at amplitude `~2.2e-4` in a breakdown of element 3720's plain local iteration, not a fold (F-789, F-796, ADR 0711, ADR 0718); with the warm start the branch continues and **folds** inside `[2.165e-4, 2.205e-4]` (F-841, ADR 0723), located by a Moore-Spence extended system (F-843, ADR 0724), and the library arc-length driver `arclength::trace_from` passes the fold on the warm model (F-797, F-845, ADR 0726, ADR 0728).
- **New opt-ins on the Newton driver** from this chain: `NewtonOptions::predictor_damping` (ADR 0688), `NewtonOptions::indefinite_tangent` (ADR 0694), `NewtonOptions::indefinite_retry` with the inertia of every accepted state (F-780, ADR 0719, done), and `arclength::trace_from` with prescribed-displacement loading (ADR 0726, partial). All are off by default.
- **Latest decision: the default local update.** F-844 (ADR 0725, done) measured each update alone as the would-be default on the eleven ADR 0658 set-ups: default 7, backtracking 8, trust region 9, warm start 11 of 11; its rule D4 recommends the warm start. F-846 (ADR 0727) counted what the switch would move: 112 of 140 switched results change bits (largest `7.8e-11`), 16 solves change outcome, all toward convergence, and four patch tests break until the element entry points accept a warm state. F-847 (ADR 0729, done) made the entry points accept a warm state by a length rule. **The switch itself is open for the maintainer** (ADR 0725 Q1, ADR 0727 Q1, ADR 0729 Q2); every stabilisation rule and remedy stays opt-in.
- **Status of the items:** 11 of the 28 items are done (F-596, F-693, F-748, F-774, F-780, F-796, F-841, F-843, F-844, F-845, F-847 by the roadmap); the rest are partial, most by a check met only after an amendment or an uncaught mutation, a few by numeric misses (ADR 0649 DB4, ADR 0666 JB1/JB2, ADR 0678 EC1/NC1, ADR 0726 AL3/AL4).

## Key numbers and limits

- Spurious zero of the plain finite-strain element under uniaxial compression: $\lambda_s = 0.60797$ (ADR 0614).
- Rule L's lowest eigenvalue in the ADR 0628 sweep stays $\ge 0.559 \mu$ in all six cases; $\Lambda_{1}$ against F-bar between `-0.55` and `+0.13` (ADR 0630, ADR 0636).
- Rule L vs F-bar punch reaction: `0.121` coarse, `0.066` refined; cantilever `0.057`, `0.019`; Full Hex8 `0.34` to `0.79` off (ADR 0636). On seven of eight distorted meshes `0.128` / `0.066` (punch), `0.069` / `0.030` (tip) (ADR 0649).
- Remedy R on DB4: $e_R = 0.0750$, $e_w = 0.0287$, positive definite (ADR 0655, ADR 0658).
- J2 three-mesh reading: F-bar order `0.75`, limit `0.0469 +- 0.0225`; rule L order `1.14` (ADR 0678). Smoothed-pressure reference $S_{\infty} = 0.017017 \pm 1.3 \times 10^{-5}$; J2 tolerance `0.07`, `0.03` on $8^3$, $16^3$ (ADR 0683).
- Bifurcation bracket `[0.964899444580, 0.964900207520]`, width `7.63e-7` after 12 bisections; critical mode orthogonal to the load to `1.5e-12` (ADR 0697). Cubic coefficient `-1.16e12`; $\mu_1 = 873.67$ (ADR 0700).
- Branch end without warm start: rung 5 at $a = 2.165 \times 10^{-4}$, $\lambda = 0.958472$; element 3720 has the smallest $l_{\mathrm{min}} / l_{\mathrm{max}}$ of the 4096 mode tangents (`1.8e-3`) but a positive-definite $K_{aa}$ ($l_{\mathrm{min}} 3.75 \times 10^{4}$) (ADR 0711, ADR 0718).
- Fold bracket with warm start `[2.165e-4, 2.205e-4]`, $a$ to `2e-3` relative, $\lambda$ to about `1e-6` (ADR 0723); fold located to $\nu_r = 3.6 \times 10^{-11}$ (ADR 0724).
- Default census: 321 driver-solve records over 34 test binaries; 112 of 140 switched results change bits, largest relative change `7.8e-11`; 16 solves change outcome (13 converge that failed, 3 with fewer halvings) (ADR 0727). A warm state holds nine more values per element (113 values against the material layout) (ADR 0729).

## Open questions

- **The default local mode update** (ADR 0725 Q1, ADR 0727 Q1, ADR 0729 Q2): (a) switch to the warm start, (b) keep it opt-in, (c) make W the default. Recommendation (a) after the entry-point follow-up, now done by ADR 0729; open for the maintainer because it changes default results, two bit records and existing tests.
- **Whether `indefinite_tangent` or the driver retry becomes a default** (ADR 0694 Q1, ADR 0700 Q3, ADR 0711 Q3, ADR 0718 Q3, ADR 0719 Q1): recommendation keep off until the CLI, the case file and the result writers show `LoadStep::inertia`. The output of `inertia` and `retried` in the solid case's report was approved on 2026-11-18 as F-842 (ADR 0719 answers), todo at the snapshot.
- **Whether a remedy may change rule L's coefficient** (ADR 0682 Q3, ADR 0694 Q2): open for the maintainer; ADR 0694 argues against it for this block.
- **Whether `trace` should accept non-zero prescribed values by default** (ADR 0726 Q3, ADR 0728 Q3): open.
- **Whether the J2 tolerance of ADR 0683 re-judges ADR 0666 JB1, JB2** (ADR 0683 Q2): open.
- **Which published stabilisation** (ADR 0614 Q1): the repository's derived penalty vs a paper's form (Wall, Bischoff and Ramm 2000; Korelc, Solinc and Wriggers 2010; Reese and Wriggers 2000); recommendation keep the derived form.
- **The fallback of the warm start** (ADR 0725 Q2): a failed warm iteration falls back to the plain iteration from 0, the one that fails at element 3720.
