# Wave

A numbered batch of work, sometimes split into lettered parts (wave 35C). Its plan and results are recorded in an ADR and in roadmap notes ([[doc-glossary]]).

## How rcfd uses it

- Older ADR titles are named by wave (e.g. ADR 0115 'Wave 45A'); newer ones by F-item and kind word ([[doc-process-agent-rules]], rule 18).
