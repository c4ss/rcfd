# Roadmap status: done, partial, not started

The three status marks of an [[f-item]]. PARTIAL (also 'partly done', `[~]`) means some of it works and its note says what is missing ([[doc-glossary]]).

## How rcfd uses it

- 'Done' is decided by the lead by the rule the item registered before its work, not by the maintainer reviewing results ([[doc-roadmap-overview]], [[doc-process-agent-rules]] Terms).
- An item whose acceptance claims agreement with a measured case is not done without a passing check of kind validation ([[doc-process-agent-rules]], rule 14).
- A diagnosed miss is an acceptable outcome, but the item is then proposed partial, not done ([[doc-process-agent-rules]], rule 8).
- "Done" is the lead's decision by the registered rule; an item whose check passed only after an amendment is done all the same when rule 2 was followed (the maintainer, 2026-11-18), ending the practice of 2026-11-13 of recording such items partial ([[doc-process-lead-process]]). Of 36 such items, 17 were marked done and 19 stayed partial ([[doc-process-documentation-review]]).
- The status is written by the [[lead]] after the push checks, not before; on 2026-11-18 the review found the opposite practice (status a median 32 s after the merge, before the checks) ([[doc-process-lead-process]], [[doc-process-documentation-review]] G9).
- The wording "done pending the maintainer's acceptance" was removed (45 places) because acceptance is per line of work, not per result ([[doc-process-documentation-review]]).
- An acceptance may be amended by the lead ("Amended <date>: ..."), the old words kept and the amendment named on the weekly list ([[doc-process-lead-process]]).
- External grading: the 2026-10-21 partials audit used DONE, PARTIAL, TODO and OVERSTATED against each item's acceptance text ([[doc-partials-audit]]); the 2026-10-14 audit proposed five done-to-partial corrections ([[doc-audit-report]]; unreviewed model output).
