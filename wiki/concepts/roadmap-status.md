# Roadmap status: done, partial, not started

The three status marks of an [[f-item]]. PARTIAL (also 'partly done', `[~]`) means some of it works and its note says what is missing ([[doc-glossary]]).

## How rcfd uses it

- 'Done' is decided by the lead by the rule the item registered before its work, not by the maintainer reviewing results ([[doc-roadmap-overview]], [[doc-process-agent-rules]] Terms).
- An item whose acceptance claims agreement with a measured case is not done without a passing check of kind validation ([[doc-process-agent-rules]], rule 14).
- A diagnosed miss is an acceptable outcome, but the item is then proposed partial, not done ([[doc-process-agent-rules]], rule 8).
