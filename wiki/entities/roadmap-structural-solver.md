# Roadmap area: Structural solver

**What it is:** the 'Structural solver' line of work (Solver family) of the [[roadmap]], with every [[f-item]] in it ([[doc-roadmap-json]]).
**Where:** `docs/roadmap.json` (source of truth), categories in `scripts/roadmap_progress.py`.

## Synthesis

Written from the first 133 ADRs of the category (ADR 0354 to 0518, 2026-11-12 to 2026-11-14; job p3-structural-a). The other 133 (ADR 0519 onward) may extend this section.

- **A large library exists and runs, all of it in `crates/rcfd-fem`.** Between 2026-11-12 and 2026-11-14 the intermediate tier (plan ADR 0290) and much of the advanced tier (plan ADR 0310) were registered and built item by item. That covers Beam2, Shell4, Shell3 and laminates ([[adr-thread-structural-beams-shells]]), cyclic symmetry, buckling and the transformation path ([[adr-thread-structural-modal-buckling-cyclic]]), rotor dynamics with Coriolis, whirl, Campbell tables and bearings ([[adr-thread-structural-rotordynamics]]), transient, harmonic, random and Craig-Bampton dynamics ([[adr-thread-structural-dynamics-harmonic]]), Newton, J2, creep, viscoelastic and orthotropic materials ([[adr-thread-structural-materials-nonlinear]]), contact ([[adr-thread-structural-contact]]), fatigue and radiated sound ([[adr-thread-structural-fatigue-and-radiated-sound]]), and sparse direct, multigrid, rank and CUDA solves ([[adr-thread-structural-linear-solvers]]).
- **The checks are verification, not validation.** The registered checks are closed forms, manufactured solutions, observed orders, patch tests, bit-identities and mutation catches ([[adr-thread-structural-verification-elements]]). No ADR in this half compares against a measured structure.
- **Many items are partial without a numeric miss.** Often every acceptance clause is met, but a planned mutation was not caught or a check was met only after an amendment written after its run. The roadmap gap then reads "The maintainer decides done" (for example F-461, F-470, F-500, F-505, F-512, F-513, F-529, F-537, F-542, F-548, F-555, F-557, F-594; see [[mutation-testing]]). The numeric known misses that remain are listed on the thread pages. Examples: F-395's buckling factor 2.0135e-2 against 2e-2, F-396's flap-lag 1.9e-3 against 1e-4 Omega^2, F-422's residual hoop stress 2.6 percent against 2 percent, F-558's I-section LTB -19.2 percent.
- **Reach from outside Rust:** the [[solid-case-file]] runs static and modal analyses through `rcfd_api::solid_case::run_solid_case_file`. No CLI subcommand and no MCP tool exist: F-405 is todo, and ADR 0438 left them to the maintainer because each changes a golden file ([[adr-thread-structural-case-file-and-fan-blade]]). The fan-blade demonstration is done (F-387). The fan-blade case-file keys were approved on 2026-11-18 (ADR 0505 answers) but were not built at the snapshot.
- **Coupling with the flow** is shown only by library checks: the elastic bar with the PISO solver (F-401, partial) and the dynamic solid as a co-simulation participant (F-400, partial). F-404 (a `[solid]` table in the flow case), F-406 and F-436 (fan demonstrations) and F-434 (public coupled benchmark) are todo ([[adr-thread-structural-fsi-coupling]]).

> [!warning] Contradiction — does a structural solver exist?
> `PRODUCT.md` ([[doc-product]]) says stress and strain colouring "needs a structural solver, which does not exist". The ADRs of this area show that a structural solver library exists. It has static, modal, buckling, dynamic and nonlinear analyses, writes stresses to `.vtu` (F-377, F-512, F-521), and has a solid case file (F-386, ADR 0438) and a fan-blade demonstration (F-387, ADR 0500). What does not exist is any route from the Studio or the tools to that library: no CLI subcommand, no MCP tool (F-405 todo, ADR 0438 Q3) and no `[solid]` table in the flow case (F-404 todo). So the PRODUCT.md sentence is false about the solver and true about what the Studio can reach today ([[adr-thread-structural-case-file-and-fan-blade]], [[doc-structural-solver]]). This is a contradiction within the repo docs.

### Second half (ADR 0519 to 0746, 2026-11-14 to 2026-11-19; job p3-structural-b)

- **The advanced tier deepened rather than widened.** The second 132 ADRs mostly take existing capabilities to more elements and paths: shells and beams into the cyclic constraint, whirl and the full rotor ([[adr-thread-structural-modal-buckling-cyclic]], [[adr-thread-structural-rotordynamics]]); hyperelastic materials onto every linear path and the structural elements ([[adr-thread-structural-hyperelastic-and-follower]]); a 14-unknown warping Beam2 on every path with load height ([[adr-thread-structural-beam-warping]]); temperature fields and Arrhenius creep on the nonlinear paths ([[adr-thread-structural-creep-and-temperature]]); quadratic and mortar contact ([[adr-thread-structural-contact]]); fracture parameters ([[adr-thread-structural-fracture]]); supernodal factors and structural smoothed aggregation ([[adr-thread-structural-linear-solvers]]).
- **One long diagnostic chain.** 28 ADRs (2026-11-15 to 2026-11-17) follow the finite-strain incompatible-modes Hex8 from a spurious hourglass zero in compression, through the stabilisation "rule L", to a confined $16^3$ punch that rule L could not solve; the cause turns out to be a subcritical bifurcation of the global path plus a breakdown of one element's local mode iteration, and the chain ends with the warm-started local update measured as the candidate default ([[adr-thread-structural-incompatible-hex8-rule-l]]).
- **Default changes were approved and made.** On 2026-11-18 the maintainer approved Beam2 in the cyclic constraint by default (made by ADR 0744) and hyperelastic materials on structural elements by default (made by ADR 0746); on 2026-11-21 lifting the remaining beam-sector opt-ins (whirl paths, `full_rotor`), not made at the snapshot ([[adr-thread-structural-modal-buckling-cyclic]], [[adr-thread-structural-hyperelastic-and-follower]]). The stagnation acceptance as a default was held ("it waits until the fan path needs it", ADR 0619) ([[adr-thread-structural-materials-nonlinear]]). The warm-start default of the incompatible Hex8 is open ([[adr-thread-structural-incompatible-hex8-rule-l]]).
- **Case-file keys approved but not built.** Under the class rule for additive golden edits (2026-11-18) the maintainer approved keys for superelements and interface enrichment (F-616), hyperelastic materials (F-621), the warping unknown (F-625), the inertia and retry of the driver in the solid case's output (F-842) and an opt-in $J$-based creep variant; all are todo at the snapshot (2e94e5ef). Since 2026-11-23 F-616 is done (ADR 0785: `[superelements.<name>]` and `[reduction]` in the solid case file, with F-889 entered for further keys of the reduction), and F-842 was registered and stopped before code because the solid case file has no nonlinear static analysis (ADR 0780, todo) ([[adr-thread-structural-case-file-and-fan-blade]]). F-850, four supporting checks of reader trial 1, is done (ADR 0778) ([[adr-thread-structural-beam-warping]]). The line counts 156 done, 127 partial, 26 not started at 09647baa ([[doc-roadmap-overview]]). The threads of the approvals: ([[adr-thread-structural-dynamics-harmonic]], [[adr-thread-structural-hyperelastic-and-follower]], [[adr-thread-structural-beam-warping]], [[adr-thread-structural-incompatible-hex8-rule-l]], [[adr-thread-structural-creep-and-temperature]]). Follower, flutter, contact, temperature, creep and shell-gradient keys are recommended and not yet approved.
- **One check against a published reference:** the hinged cylindrical roof limit load against Sze, Liu and Lo (2004), `2226.3 N`, Shell4 `1.8e-3` and Shell3 `2.3e-3` below it (ADR 0716). Every other check in this half is a closed form, a derived semi-analytical reference, a twin model or a bit record; none compares against a measured structure ([[adr-thread-structural-beams-shells]]).
- **The PRODUCT.md question, from this half.** The second half confirms the contradiction above: it adds a whole nonlinear, contact, fracture and rotor library and names "the solid case's output" as an approved target (F-842, ADR 0719). What stays true is the Studio side: the Studio's capability inventory ([apps/studio-web/capabilities/inventory.json](../../apps/studio-web/capabilities/inventory.json)) lists only flow-case keys, none of the solid case, and the Studio sources under `apps/` have no stress or strain view, so the Studio has no stress or strain colouring at the snapshot (checked by text search in the snapshot for this ingest, not stated by an ADR).
