# Roadmap area: Quality, docs and validation

**What it is:** the 'Quality, docs and validation' line of work (Project family) of the [[roadmap]], with every [[f-item]] in it ([[doc-roadmap-json]]).
**Where:** `docs/roadmap.json` (source of truth), categories in `scripts/roadmap_progress.py`.

## Synthesis

Three kinds of work share this line: validation headlines, the reliability of the full gate, and the lead's tooling.

- **Validation headlines.** F-865 (ADR 0756) made per cent complete per simulation milestone; its own reading is that the per cents run high and "The column 'Headline result' is the one to read". The maintainer's answers of 2026-11-21 created three headline items. F-868 (NACA 0012 lift, ADR 0760) ~~is partial~~: the lift is within max(5 %, 0.018) of Ladson from 0 to 16 degrees, and 17 degrees is a known miss. An unsteady arm was approved and issued as ADR 0764 ([[adr-thread-simulation-milestones-validation]]). Since 5b96a1f8 F-868 is **done**: the time-accurate arm of ADR 0764 settles at 17 degrees to a steady attached flow with $c_l$ 1.5516, within the band 0.0803 of Ladson's 1.6057, and the maintainer read it as meeting the acceptance (option (a)); the steady route at 17 degrees is the new F-884 ([[doc-roadmap-json]]). F-869 (cylinder drag, ADRs 0761, 0763) ~~is partial~~: the fine-mesh drag 1.33900 is in [1.314, 1.370], but the observed order 1.387 is outside [1.5, 2.5]; ~~ADR 0763 question 1 is with the maintainer~~ ([[adr-thread-simulation-milestones-validation]]). Since 5b96a1f8 F-869 is **done** "as it stands, the order miss kept as a known miss" (maintainer, option (c)); the diagnosis of the order is F-881, and the vortex-shedding headline reads "met" ([[doc-roadmap-json]], [[doc-roadmap-overview]]). F-870 (FAN-01 design point, ADR 0762) is partial: the pressure rise is 73.99 Pa against 126.46 Pa and the shaft power is within 2.0 %. The band cannot be completed without the measurement's uncertainty, which is follow-up F-877 and needs a person to read it ([[adr-thread-fan01-design-point]]). F-867 (ADR 0757) made the fan milestone the whole design goal ([[adr-thread-simulation-milestones-validation]]). At 5b96a1f8 the F-870 acceptance also compares the Euler head, the kinetic energy leaving the rotor and the circumferential-mean $c_x$, $c_t$ profiles at the measured plane (ADR 0765, question 1), and its convergence rule waits on F-885 ([[doc-roadmap-json]]). **At 09647baa (2026-11-23):** F-884 (ADR 0770) and F-887 (ADR 0772) are done: the steady Spalart-Allmaras route reaches the attached state at 17 degrees with `turbulence.relax_nut = 1.0`, and all twelve angles hold with it; no default is changed. F-881 (ADR 0771) is done: the cylinder's order 1.387 comes from the first-order wall terms of the force integral. F-871 (ADR 0775) and F-875 (ADR 0779) are done: ten milestones, among them `fan-structure` ([[adr-thread-simulation-milestones-validation]]). On FAN-01, F-883 is done (a measured casing pressure, now a compared quantity of F-870), F-882 and F-885 are partial, and none of the three decides the missing pressure rise or the convergence rule ([[adr-thread-fan01-design-point]]).
- **Gate reliability.** The daily full gate runs in CI with failures attributed to ADRs (F-723, ADR 0653), and scheduled runs have used four shards since 2026-11-21. The failures it found were fixed one item at a time: per-test time limits (F-849), wall-clock limits on a loaded runner (F-854), a shared temporary folder (F-853) and a negative control (F-866). The [[edit-lock]] needed four items (F-751, F-755, F-761, F-855), and F-855 stays partial until a hosted gate shows F2 passing or failing with a printed class ([[adr-thread-daily-gate-and-edit-lock]]).
