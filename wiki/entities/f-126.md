# F-126 (turbomachinery rotating interfaces, the hub item of the fan milestone)

**What it is:** the roadmap item "Turbomachinery rotating interfaces: frozen-rotor and stage (mixing-plane) interfaces with sector periodicity" in the moving-meshes area, and the item the `fan` milestone hangs on ([[doc-roadmap-json]], [[adr-thread-fan01-rotating-solve]]). Its acceptance is "a public compressor case reproducing pressure ratio and efficiency within published scatter" ([[roadmap-moving-meshes-rotors-and-coupling]]).
**Status at 2e94e5ef:** partial. The acceptance "is not attempted"; also missing are "MRF on the bladed FAN-01 passage (blade wall refused by the revolution check), compressible mixed-out state, rothalpy, a downstream-velocity constraint, and transient and coupled solvers" ([[roadmap-moving-meshes-rotors-and-coupling]]).

## Details

- **What exists.** A frozen-rotor (MRF) zone in the steady SIMPLE kernel, checked on analytic flows only (ADR 0008) ([[adr-thread-fan01-rotating-solve]]); a frame-transformation and interface-diagnostics library and a mixing-plane library (ADR 0055), the mixing plane in the steady loop between two unjoined annulus blocks (ADR 0059); a cylindrical non-conformal rotor/stator join with a 70 deg pre-flight and the case-tree generator `annulus_3d_cylindrical_interface` (ADRs 0109, 0112); MRF zones with rotationally cyclic seams (`sources.<name>.cyclic_seams`, ADR 0113) and an exact revolution-walled sector `annulus_3d_sector` that takes them through the case tree (ADR 0116) ([[adr-thread-fan01-rotating-solve]]). The transient sliding rotor-stator path runs from a case file on bladeless annuli and sectors (F-303, F-711) ([[adr-thread-sliding-mesh-rotor-stator]]). See [[mrf]], [[sliding-mesh]].
- **The acceptance attempt on FAN-01.** ADR 0075 chose the FAU / EAA fan as the best-fitting public case and registered three limits per operating point (pressure rise within "3 Pa + 0.03 measured", torque within 5 %, efficiency within 0.03). The registered attempt was NOT RUNNABLE, attempt 2 NOT CONVERGED, and attempt 3 "converged and MISSED every limit by a wide margin" (torque 1.320 N m against 2.142 N m, Delta p_ts -3.8 Pa against 126.5 Pa) ([[adr-thread-fan01-rotating-solve]]). See [[fan-01]].
- **The diagnosis that followed** (ADRs 0078 to 0117, wave 35 to 45): the deficit is mostly numerical swirl manufactured by the momentum convection of the relative velocity (ADRs 0078, 0082); the first solution verification gives bands "not shown credible on this family" (ADR 0086); the steady run stalls in a limit cycle of the limited `linear_upwind` with no attributed mechanism (ADRs 0110, 0111, 0115); MRF itself is blocked on the bladed passage because "the blade blocks MRF whatever the hub and shroud are" (ADR 0116) ([[adr-thread-fan01-rotating-solve]]).
- **Design-point check.** F-870 (ADR 0762, 2026-11-22) puts the rotating-frame solve at 73.988 Pa against 126.46 Pa at the design point, with shaft power within 2.0 % ([[adr-thread-fan01-design-point]]). Its follow-ups of 2026-11-22: ADR 0765 (F-878) compares the solve with the measured LDA profiles (12 of 26 radii miss in each component; the Euler work is close to the measured) and ADR 0766 (F-879) runs check H of ADR 0183 in time (does not decide); F-882, F-883 and F-885 are entered ([[adr-thread-fan01-design-point]]).
- **Defaults held for it.** On 2026-11-18 the maintainer held several default changes "until the fan path needs it" (F-721, F-724, F-364, F-365, F-775) ([[adr-thread-fan01-rotating-solve]]).
