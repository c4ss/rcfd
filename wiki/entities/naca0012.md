# NACA 0012 aerofoil

**What it is:** the NACA 0012 at Re 6e6 in incompressible steady RANS on an O-grid, compared by the lift curve up to maximum lift against Ladson's measured lift; the case behind the `aerofoil-lift` milestone (headline F-043, F-868) ([[doc-milestones-json]]).
**Where:** `cases/naca0012/case.json`, `cases/naca0012/reference/naca4_thickness.txt`, AoA-sweep scripts and tests, `crates/rcfd-api/tests/naca0012_lift_curve_f868.rs`; dataset `naca0012-tmr` ([[doc-validation-data]]).

## Details

- Geometry: y_t = 5t(0.2969 sqrt x - 0.1260 x - 0.3516 x^2 + 0.2843 x^3 - 0.1036 x^4) (closed trailing edge; the open one uses -0.1015), from Wikipedia, verified; thin-aerofoil C_L = 2 pi alpha = 0.1097 per degree ([[doc-reference-data-naca0012-thickness]]).
- Data (wave 31C): NASA TMR page, Re 6e6 per chord, M 0.15; Ladson tripped force data (NASA TM 4074, 1988) `CLCD_Ladson_expdata.dat` (80/120/180 grit); Gregory and O'Reilly Cp; files already in the F-125 pack (CC0). Observations: "Cl slope about 0.108 per degree below 6.1 degrees (thin airfoil 0.1097), Cd min 0.00800 (80 grit), Cl max 1.6116 at 17.13 degrees" ([[doc-reference-data-wave31c-reference-reading]]).
- F-868 (ADR 0760), read 2026-11-22: file 1343 bytes, sha256 `78cd2f6aa4968e80f44cbf6c96f699bd9c6e45681d958ec528a10cf72ed23357`. The 120-grit zone has its maximum (17.24, 1.6347) and the 180-grit zone (17.13, 1.6219). Scatter of the three zones from 0 to 17 degrees ranges 0.0028 to 0.0177 (0.2635 at 18 degrees, beyond maximum lift); "the value taken is 0.018". "The report NASA TM 4074 itself was not read. The file states no uncertainty" ([[doc-reference-data-f868-ladson-naca0012]]).

- **F-868 result (ADR 0760, 2026-11-22, partial):** steady SA on M1w-far500 (ADR 0167's SA arm, reproduced bit for bit) puts the lift within the band max(5 %, 0.018) of Ladson's 80-grit lift at 0, 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 13, 14, 15 and 16 degrees. The largest difference is 0.0143 at 16, and all eleven are within the scatter 0.018. The maximum is 1.542, 4.3 % under the measured 1.6116. 17 degrees is a known miss: there is no steady value, and the iteration mean is 1.0476 against 1.6057 ([[adr-thread-simulation-milestones-validation]]).
- **17 degrees by an unsteady arm (ADR 0764, 2026-11-22; F-868 done):** Spalart-Allmaras without DES on `pimple`, BDF2, dt 0.02 c/U to t = 200 on the same mesh M1w-far500 settles to a steady attached flow with mean $c_l$ 1.5516 over [100, 200], 0.0541 (3.4 %) under Ladson's 1.6057 and within the unchanged band 0.0803; nothing sheds (range 0.0012). The control at 15 degrees gives 1.49167 against the steady 1.4932 ([[adr-thread-simulation-milestones-validation]]). The maintainer accepted a steady state reached by a time-accurate run as meeting the acceptance (option (a)); the steady SIMPLEC iteration still does not reach that state, which is F-884 (todo), and the known miss of ADR 0760 stays as the record of the steady arm ([[adr-thread-simulation-milestones-validation]]). The computed maximum over the compared angles becomes 1.5516 at 17 degrees, 3.7 % under the measured 1.6116 ([[adr-thread-simulation-milestones-validation]]).
- The test is `crates/rcfd-api/tests/naca0012_lift_curve_f868.rs`, with two negative controls and a fast run on the coarse example mesh. 18 to 24 degrees, drag and moment are not compared ([[adr-thread-simulation-milestones-validation]]).
- Later registrations against this measured set take the band 0.018 below stall, not max(5 %, 0.018). This is the lead's decision on ADR 0760 question 2 ([[adr-thread-simulation-milestones-validation]]).

> [!warning] Contradiction
> The F-868 roadmap gap and [[doc-roadmap-overview]] say 17 degrees waits on "the maintainer's word that the headline ends at 16 degrees (ADR 0760, question 1)". ADR 0760's "Answers of the maintainer (2026-11-22)" settle that question with option (a): F-868 stays partial and an unsteady arm at 17 degrees is issued as ADR 0764 ([[adr-thread-simulation-milestones-validation]]). The contradiction is within the repo docs, because the gap text predates the answer. Resolved at origin/main 5b96a1f8: F-868 is done (ADR 0764, answer (a)) and [[doc-roadmap-overview]] lists it as met ([[adr-thread-simulation-milestones-validation]]).
- **The investigation chain behind F-868 (2026-10-29 to 2026-10-30).** Job 48Y (ADR 0159), 50-chord M1w O-grid at y+ about 40: SA slope 0.920 x 2 pi, peak 1.5278 at 20 degrees (about 3 degrees late), SST peak 1.3973 at 14 degrees (about 3 degrees early), SA drag "1.4 to 4.6 times Ladson's drag" from 4 degrees ([[doc-investigation-job48y-aoa-sweep]]). Job 49A (ADR 0164) found two causes: the molecular force report at y+ 40 (fix `total_effective`, cd at 0 degrees +3.6 % against TMR) and the far-field nuTilda growing to 6.0e4 nu (fix far-field role `freestream`) ([[doc-investigation-job49a-sa-drag]]). Job 49D (ADR 0167) re-ran the sweep with both fixes on 500 chords: slope 0.986 x 2 pi, cl_max 1.542 at 16 degrees, LT15 (cd +26.0 % at 15 degrees, band 25 %) and LD known misses ([[doc-investigation-job49d-aoa-sweep-fixed]]); F-868 reproduces that curve bit for bit ([[doc-investigation-f868-naca0012-lift-curve]]). Job 49E (ADR 0168): SA `auto` wall mode +17.8 % in cd_eff at median wall y+ 5.4, opt-in `auto_sa_law` +2.0 % ([[doc-investigation-job49e-sa-wall]]). Job 49H (ADR 0171): unlimited `linear_upwind` diverges in the trailing-edge tip cells of the y+ 2 O-grid at 6 degrees; `barth_jespersen` removes it ([[doc-investigation-job49h-te-divergence]]).
- The Studio example [[example-project-wind-tunnel]] runs the NACA 0012 with k-omega SST at 4 and 2 degrees on ADR 0092's coarse 6144-cell O-grid (cl 0.3469 and 0.1982), "not a validation" ([[doc-example-projects]]).
- **Jobs 48Y to 49H (2026-10-29 to 2026-10-30):** ADR 0159 swept 0 to 24 degrees through `rcfd run`; the registered mesh M1 diverged at its trailing-edge cells, so the counted runs used M1w (wall y+ about 40); "SA's drag is half the measured value at 0 degrees and 1.4 to 4.6 times it from 4 degrees up". ADR 0164 found the two causes (the molecular force report, 0.319 of the applied shear; the unanchored far-field nuTilda), and ADRs 0166, 0169 and 0170 made the effective wall shear and the `freestream` far-field role the defaults. ADR 0167's repeat on a 500-chord far field (lift slope 0.986 x 2 pi, peak 1.542 at 16 degrees, cd +2.5 % / +9.8 % / +26.0 % against TMR at 0 / 10 / 15 degrees) is the arm F-868 reproduces ([[adr-thread-naca0012-sa-drag]]).
- The trailing-edge divergence at fine wall spacing (M1, first cell 1.57e-5 chord, 6 degrees, guard at iteration 15 in tip cell 0) is removed by the existing opt-in `solver.linear_upwind_limiter = "barth_jespersen"`, at +3.0 % of `cd_eff` at 0 degrees; not made the default (ADR 0171, [[adr-thread-naca0012-sa-drag]]). The static warning SV302 flags the risk on such meshes ([[adr-thread-setup-warnings-and-time-step]]).
