# NACA 0012 aerofoil

**What it is:** the NACA 0012 at Re 6e6 in incompressible steady RANS on an O-grid, compared by the lift curve up to maximum lift against Ladson's measured lift; the case behind the `aerofoil-lift` milestone (headline F-043, F-868) ([[doc-milestones-json]]).
**Where:** `cases/naca0012/case.json`, `cases/naca0012/reference/naca4_thickness.txt`, AoA-sweep scripts and tests, `crates/rcfd-api/tests/naca0012_lift_curve_f868.rs`; dataset `naca0012-tmr` ([[doc-validation-data]]).

## Details

- Geometry: y_t = 5t(0.2969 sqrt x - 0.1260 x - 0.3516 x^2 + 0.2843 x^3 - 0.1036 x^4) (closed trailing edge; the open one uses -0.1015), from Wikipedia, verified; thin-aerofoil C_L = 2 pi alpha = 0.1097 per degree ([[doc-reference-data-naca0012-thickness]]).
- Data (wave 31C): NASA TMR page, Re 6e6 per chord, M 0.15; Ladson tripped force data (NASA TM 4074, 1988) `CLCD_Ladson_expdata.dat` (80/120/180 grit); Gregory and O'Reilly Cp; files already in the F-125 pack (CC0). Observations: "Cl slope about 0.108 per degree below 6.1 degrees (thin airfoil 0.1097), Cd min 0.00800 (80 grit), Cl max 1.6116 at 17.13 degrees" ([[doc-reference-data-wave31c-reference-reading]]).
- F-868 (ADR 0760), read 2026-11-22: file 1343 bytes, sha256 `78cd2f6aa4968e80f44cbf6c96f699bd9c6e45681d958ec528a10cf72ed23357`. The 120-grit zone has its maximum (17.24, 1.6347) and the 180-grit zone (17.13, 1.6219). Scatter of the three zones from 0 to 17 degrees ranges 0.0028 to 0.0177 (0.2635 at 18 degrees, beyond maximum lift); "the value taken is 0.018". "The report NASA TM 4074 itself was not read. The file states no uncertainty" ([[doc-reference-data-f868-ladson-naca0012]]).
